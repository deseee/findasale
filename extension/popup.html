Fills and publishes each listing automatically, pausing if anything looks off. Uncheck to fill but stop before Publish so you can review first. (Chrome's “debugging this tab” banner is normal.)

When an item sells elsewhere, the extension removes its Facebook listing (checked every ~20 min). “Remove automatically” runs in a background tab; “Notify me” waits for you to click the alert first.
