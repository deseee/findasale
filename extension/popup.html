FindA.Sale Marketplace Autofill
0 selected

Opens Craigslist and fills each posting (for sale by owner) — title, price, description, and photos. You choose the location, complete any verification, and click publish yourself.

Fills and publishes each listing automatically, pausing if anything looks off. Uncheck to fill but stop before Publish so you can review first. (Chrome's “debugging this tab” banner is normal.)

Checked every ~20 min. When an item sells elsewhere, the extension removes its Facebook listing — “Remove automatically” runs in a background tab, “Notify me” waits for you to click the alert first. When you change an item's price on FindA.Sale, you'll also get a notification to update it on Facebook yourself — automatic price updates aren't built yet, so this always just notifies for now, even in “Remove automatically” mode. “Off” turns off both.
