# The True Plan — FindA.Sale 90-Day Organizer Acquisition Playbook

*Updated: 2026-04-16 | Owner: Patrick | No hedging. Commit to the sequence.*

---

## The One Goal

**10 paying organizers by Day 30. 50 by Day 90.**

Not 10 "trials." Not 10 "leads." Ten organizers who have created a sale, listed items, and paid for at least one month. That's proof the engine works. Everything in this plan is in service of that number.

---

## The Logic

The research produced nine approved ideas, eight guru frameworks, and eleven influencer targets. That's too many levers to pull at once. Here's how they stack:

**Tier 1 — Week 1 (Do this week. No excuses. No budget required.)**
Direct outreach to five real organizers. No tools. No automation. Just Patrick on the phone. This is Kagan's 48-hour validation rule and Hormozi's "do things that don't scale first." The goal is five conversations, two trials started.

**Tier 2 — Month 1 (Scale what worked in Week 1 with tools.)**
Outscraper + RVM + cold email. Koerner methodology applied to the full Grand Rapids secondary sale market. Community presence (GMB, Nextdoor, local Facebook groups). ASEL professional association outreach. Content flywheel started.

**Tier 3 — Month 2–3 (Compound the flywheel.)**
Shopper-side influencer pull (Airbnb model). Podcast launch. Probate attorney referral network. EstateSales.NET migration play. Month-2 upsell email automation. By now the product should be speaking for itself.

## Week 1: The Concierge Sprint

*Goal: 5 organizer conversations. 2+ trials. 1 case study started.*

### Day 1–2: Find Your Five

Patrick personally identifies five sale organizers in Grand Rapids who match the ICP:
- Solo or 2-person operation
- Run 6–20 sales per year
- Currently using spreadsheets, phone photos, Venmo
- Tech-comfortable (smartphone, uses Facebook/Instagram)
- Frustrated by setup time

Sources:
- **Facebook Groups:** "Grand Rapids Garage Sales," "West Michigan Estate Sales," "GR Flea Market." Organizers post their own sales — they're already there. Send a personal message.
- **EstateSales.NET / EstateSales.org:** Organizers listed publicly with contact info. Find the West Michigan listings and contact the organizer directly.
- **Nextdoor:** Search "estate sale" or "yard sale" — organizers who post there are exactly the ICP.
- **Google Maps:** "estate sale company grand rapids" → organizers with a business listing.

Script (keep it this short):

> "Hey [Name], I'm Patrick — I built a free app for sale organizers in Grand Rapids called FindA.Sale. I'm talking to 5 organizers this week to make sure it actually solves real problems. Would you be open to a 15-minute call? I'm not selling anything — I just want to make sure the tool is useful before I launch it locally."

### Day 3–5: Run the Conversations

Goal per call:
1. Ask what's most painful about running a sale today (don't pitch first)
2. Show the photo-to-listing demo (one photo → complete listing in 30 seconds)
3. Offer free access: "I'll set it up for your next sale, you pay nothing until you decide it's worth it"
4. If they bite: onboard them personally. Help them create their first sale.

### Day 5–7: Document One Story

Pick the most engaged organizer. Get a before/after:
- "How long did inventory used to take?"
- "How long did it take with FindA.Sale?"
- Ask for a quote you can use

This becomes the foundation of every piece of content in Month 1.

**Budget: $0. Owner: Patrick.**
**Success metric: 5 calls completed, 2+ trials started, 1 case study quote in hand.**

## Month 1: Scale the Engine

*Goal: 20+ organizers on platform. 5+ paying. First month of real revenue.*

### Channel 1: Outscraper + RVM + Cold Email (Koerner Methodology)

**Step 1 — Build the list:**
Use Outscraper ($49/mo) to pull Google Maps data for:
- estate sale company, estate sale organizer, estate liquidator
- auction house, auction company, estate auctioneer
- consignment shop, resale shop
- flea market organizer, yard sale organizer

Pull Grand Rapids + 50-mile radius first. Expect 200–500 contacts with name, phone, email.
Clean with Searchbug ($19/mo) — remove landlines, validate emails.
Output: 150–400 contactable organizers.

**Step 2 — RVM drop (Slybroadcast, $0.06/drop):**
Record a 20-second voicemail. Patrick's voice. No script sound.

> "Hey, this is Patrick — I built an app for sale organizers in the Grand Rapids area called FindA.Sale. It takes a photo of an item and creates the full listing automatically. No typing, no spreadsheets. If you're running sales in West Michigan, I'd love to show you a demo. Text me back or check it out at finda.sale."

Drop to the full list. Cost: ~$9–24 for 150–400 drops.
Response rate expectation: 1–3% reply. That's 2–12 conversations from one message.

**Step 3 — Cold email sequence (Dan Henry B2B style):**
Email 1 (Day 1, same day as RVM): short, personal, one ask.

> Subject: Sale organizers in Grand Rapids
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm Patrick, a Grand Rapids developer who built a tool specifically for sale organizers. It lets you photograph items and have them fully listed — title, description, price, category — in about 10 seconds per item.
>
> I'm doing free onboarding for local organizers this month. Worth a 15-minute call?
>
> [Calendar link]
>
> Patrick

Email 2 (Day 4): "Quick follow-up — did you get a chance to see this?"
Email 3 (Day 10): "Last note — just sharing a quick before/after from an organizer I worked with this week." (Include the Week 1 case study.)

**Step 4 — VA follow-up:**
Hire one part-time VA on Upwork ($10–15/hr, 5–10 hrs/week) to:
- Monitor RVM responses and reply
- Handle inbound email replies with a script Patrick provides
- Schedule demos directly to Patrick's calendar

**Total Month 1 channel cost: ~$200–350.** (Outscraper + Searchbug + RVM + VA hours)

Comment helpfully on posts where organizers ask questions ("what do you use for inventory?" → "I built something for exactly this — happy to show you"). Post one useful tip per week. ### Probate Attorney Referral Network

Probate attorneys handle estates. Estate executors need to liquidate physical assets. The connection: attorneys are natural referral partners because they work with estate families before anyone thinks to run a sale.

**Action:**
1. List the top 20 probate attorneys in West Michigan (Justia, FindLaw, Google)
2. Send a personal email: "I built a platform that helps estate executors run sales without hiring a liquidation company. I'm looking for 2–3 attorneys to refer clients to — in exchange, I'll give referred organizers 3 free months and credit you on their profile."
3. Create the referral tracking link (the Month 1 code task)
4. When one says yes, ask for an introduction to their local estate planning bar association chapter

This channel has a 30–90 day sales cycle but the referrals convert at high rates — attorneys only refer what they trust.

## What We Are NOT Doing (In This 90-Day Window)

- **Paid ads (Facebook/Google):** Not until organic channels are saturated and we have a proven conversion rate to optimize against. Ads amplify what already works — we don't have proof yet.
- **National launch:** Grand Rapids first. Prove the model. Then expand.
- **Enterprise/liquidation companies:** Wrong ICP. They have their own software. Our target is solo + small teams.
- **Building features:** The product is feature-complete for the ICP. Ship what's in the Month 1 code tasks list and nothing else. Every hour of dev is an hour not spent on customer conversations.

## The Decision Stack

When something feels important but isn't on this list, run it through this filter:

1. Does it get an organizer on the phone this week? → Do it.
2. Does it help an organizer have a successful first sale? → Do it.
3. Does it create content that reaches other organizers? → Do it.
4. Does it build a referral channel? → Queue for Month 2.
5. Everything else → Defer to Day 90 review.
