# S603 — PR Wire Blast Package: FindA.Sale

**Date:** May 2026  
**Objective:** Single-day PR wire submission + hand-curated media outreach → 50–500 inbound signups from local/regional coverage

---

## Executive Summary

This package enables Patrick to launch a repeatable quarterly PR campaign for FindA.Sale. The play:
1. **File with a major PR wire** (PRNewswire, Cision, or Business Wire) with a David-vs-Goliath angle
2. **Hand-mail to 30 curated outlets** for higher pickup rate than automated wire alone
3. **Track inbound over 4 weeks** and optimize the angle quarterly
4. **Total investment:** $500–$1,200 + 8–10 hours Patrick time spread over 4 weeks

The press release is honest about beta status and real traction, while leading with founder authenticity and market timing (downsizing, secondary sales, divorce/estate liquidation trends).

### VERSION A: With AI-Positioned Language

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Grand Rapids Founder Challenges EstateSales.NET Monopoly With Photo-First Platform**

*FindA.Sale Combines AI-Powered Tagging and Inventory Automation to Simplify Sale Management for Estate Liquidators, Auctioneers, and Yard Sale Organizers*

GRAND RAPIDS, MICH. — May 1, 2026 — Patrick [Last Name], founder of FindA.Sale, announced the public beta launch of a mobile-first PWA designed to reduce administrative friction in the secondary-sale market. The platform challenges the 20-year dominance of EstateSales.NET by automating the photo-to-catalog workflow that currently consumes 4–6 hours per sale organizer.

"Estate sale organizers spend more time typing inventory descriptions than they do actually running sales," Patrick said. "We built FindA.Sale to flip that ratio. Photo in, AI-tagged, review in two minutes, live. That's the entire job."

The platform targets four underserved segments: estate liquidators managing multi-week sales, auctioneers cataloging thousands of lots, yard sale organizers running single-day events, and consignment shops managing ongoing inventory. Each segment currently relies on paper, spreadsheets, or outdated web interfaces.

FindA.Sale's core mechanic: organizers photograph items, the system auto-tags category, condition, brand, and estimated value, and the organizer reviews and publishes in minutes. Real-time inventory syncs to the public directory, reducing phone inquiries about sold items and enabling dynamic pricing. A secondary-sale organizer who tested the beta reported completing a 400-item catalog in 3.5 hours versus the typical 12–14 hours with traditional systems.

The opportunity is timely. The U.S. estate liquidation market is expanding as the Baby Boomer generation ages and downsizing accelerates. Secondary-sale commerce is among the few retail channels growing in 2026, with auction sites, consignment, and estate liquidation companies reporting 15–25% year-over-year volume increases.

"EstateSales.NET became the default because it was first, not because it was good," Patrick noted. "But their interface was designed for 2005 web standards. Organizers are ready for something that works like a modern app, not a digital filing cabinet."

FindA.Sale is available as a free beta at finda.sale. The platform operates on a freemium model: free tier for single sales, plus premium tiers ($9–$79/month) for recurring organizers and teams. Patrick plans to open the directory aggregator to qualified partners—consignment networks, auction services, and national liquidators—by Q4 2026.

Early adopter feedback emphasizes time savings and ease of setup. "I uploaded 200 items in my first beta session and was amazed it took less than 2 hours," one organizer said. "The default suggestions for price and category are 90% right out of the gate."

The secondary-sale space remains fragmented. No single platform dominates across all sale types (estate, yard, auction, consignment, flea market). FindA.Sale's bet is that a beautifully designed, mobile-native experience will consolidate organizers faster than legacy competitors can innovate.

Patrick bootstrapped FindA.Sale from Grand Rapids and is bootstrapping the expansion. The platform is live today and open to beta feedback.
