## Send Strategy

### Recommended Sequence (Cold → Warm → Hot)

**Week 1 — Trade Associations (Low Risk, Slow Response):**  
Send NESA, NAA ×2, NASMM, ISA. Expect 30–60 day response window for membership orgs.

**Week 2 — Podcast/Newsletter Voices (Medium Warm):**  
Send Estate Professionals, The Auction Advocate, Downsizing Roadmap, Empowered Elder, Grace Rehman, Consignment Chats, Probate Realtor, Antiques Diva. These have direct reach to organizers and shorter decision cycles (7–14 days typical).

**Week 3 — Influencers & Content Creators (Higher Value):**  
Send Nick Loper, Codie Sanchez. These are individuals with agency; send after you have at least 2–3 positive responses from Weeks 1–2 to cite in follow-up.

**Week 4 — Educational Platforms (Case Studies & Integrations):**  
Send Pat Walls, Junk Removal Authority, Senior Settlers, SRES Template (deploy to 3–5 individual targets). These have longer sales cycles and benefit from social proof.

### Spacing & Rhythm

- Send **one email per day** from Patrick's personal email (deseee@yahoo.com)
- Do NOT batch-send multiple emails in one session — it signals mass outreach
- Stagger by geography when possible (East Coast morning, then Midwest afternoon, then West)
- Space 24–48 hours between related sends (e.g., NAA ×2)

### Response Handling

**If positive (interest in call / meeting):**
- Reply within 24 hours
- Offer 3 concrete time slots for the next 10 days
- Keep reply brief — they clicked because they're interested, not because they want more copy
- After call, send brief follow-up with any resources discussed + next steps

**If no response after 10 days:**
- Single follow-up email (different angle, no sales pressure)
- Example: "Been thinking about [NESA] members and [specific pain point] — wonder if there's a different person on your team who owns partnerships"
- If still no response after 14 days total, mark as "cold" and move on

**If declining / negative:**
- Reply with gratitude
- Ask one question about their feedback (you want to learn, not pitch)
- Save the email for future reference — patterns across declines are valuable

### Voice Guardrails for Replies

- No exclamation marks
- No corporate enthusiasm ("excited," "thrilled," "pumped")
- No feature lists in replies
- Always ask questions about their business/audience before talking about ours
- If they ask "what does it do," keep answer to 2–3 sentences; let them ask follow-ups
