# Refer a Friend, Earn XP and Coupons

Tell a friend about FindA.Sale and you both get rewarded. Share your referral link, they sign up and make a purchase, and you earn 100 XP plus a coupon.

## How referrals work

**You share a link.** From your profile, grab your unique referral link and text or email it to a friend.

**They sign up.** Your friend clicks the link, creates an account, and buys something from a sale.

**You get rewarded.** Once their purchase completes, you earn 100 XP and a $5 coupon. They get a $5 coupon too.

**They get the full experience.** Your friend isn't getting a gimped version — they get the whole app, the same deals, everything. You're just introducing them.

## What you earn

- **100 XP per referral** (when they make their first purchase)
- **$5 coupon** (instantly)
- **Achievement credit** (Referral badges unlock at 3, 10, and 25 referrals)

If you refer 10 friends and 7 of them make purchases, you earn 700 XP and 7 coupons. That's solid.

## How to share your referral link

**From your profile:**
1. Go to your profile
2. Tap "Referral"
3. Copy your unique link
4. Send via text, email, social media, whatever

**From the dashboard:**
Some pages have a "Invite a Friend" widget. Tap it and share from there.

Your referral link is unique to you. Anyone who signs up through your link is credited to you.

## For your friends

Your friends get a $5 coupon when they sign up through your link (even before they make a purchase). Then, when they complete their first purchase, both of you get bonuses.

They're not obligated to use the app or spend money. The coupon is theirs to use or ignore. But if they try it out, they'll probably find something cool.

## Coupons vs. referrals

Referrals are about converting friends. Coupons are the incentive. Your friend gets a coupon, browses the app, maybe finds a sale nearby, uses the coupon, and is now a shopper. Win for them, win for you.
