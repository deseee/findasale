# Streak Rewards: Daily Logins and How to Keep Them Going

A streak is a consecutive run of daily logins. The longer your streak, the better the rewards. Keep showing up and you'll unlock bonus XP, coupons, and bragging rights.

## How streaks work

Every time you open FindA.Sale and check in, you add one day to your streak. Check in the next day — streak continues. Miss a day — streak resets to zero.

Your current streak appears on your profile and in the main dashboard widget. It shows today's number and yesterday's count, so you always know where you stand.

## Streak milestones and rewards

The longer you go, the better the payoff.

| Streak | Reward |
|--------|--------|
| 3 days | 10 XP bonus |
| 7 days | 50 XP bonus + $2 coupon |
| 14 days | 100 XP bonus + $5 coupon |
| 30 days | 200 XP bonus + $10 coupon + Streak badge |
| 60 days | 400 XP bonus + $20 coupon |
| 90 days | 600 XP bonus + $30 coupon + "Committed" achievement |

Rewards trigger automatically when you hit the milestone. The XP goes straight to your account and the coupon lands in your wallet the same day.

## Why streaks matter

Streaks are partly about XP, but mostly about habit. Shoppers who check in daily see:

- New sales posted every morning
- Early notifications about inventory drops
- Better reach on their own haul posts (the algorithm favors active shoppers)
- Stronger community presence (your name appears more often in the feed)

A 30-day streak also counts as an achievement, which gives you bragging rights on your profile.

## Breaking your streak

If you miss a day, your streak resets. There's no way to buy back a missed day or extend a streak retroactively. It's all-or-nothing.

**Pro tip:** Set a daily reminder on your phone to check in. Even 30 seconds of opening the app counts. You don't have to hunt or spend money — just check in.

## Streaks and the leaderboard

Your active streak influences your placement on the monthly leaderboard. Long streaks combined with high XP gains make you visible to other shoppers and organizers.

## What happens if you're traveling

If you're out of service or traveling without cell service, your streak will break. There's no workaround for true offline periods, but if you're just busy — take 30 seconds to check in whenever you can.

Some shoppers set repeating calendar alerts the night before as a backup reminder.
