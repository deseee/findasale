# Loot Log: Track Everything You've Bought

Every item you buy at a sale—whether you pay in person or through FindA.Sale—gets logged in your Loot Log. It's your personal history, your budget tracker, and your collection inventory all in one place.

## How it works

1. **Browse your history** — Tap your profile > Loot Log. You'll see all purchases, sorted by date (newest first).
2. **See the details** — For each item: the sale name, item, price paid, date bought, condition grade, and organizer.
3. **Filter by date** — Use the date picker to see purchases from a specific week, month, or year.
4. **Filter by type** — See only purchases from yard sales, consignment, auctions, or flea markets.
5. **Search by item** — Search "dresser" or "vintage" to find items you've bought before.
6. **View total spent** — The top of the page shows your total spending this week, month, or year.
7. **Add a note** — Tap an item and add a personal note: "Refinished it dark walnut" or "Sold for $200 on eBay." Great for tracking resale or DIY projects.
8. **Mark as sold** — If you resell something, mark it as sold. The app tracks your profit or loss.

## Why use the Loot Log

**Budget tracking** — See how much you've spent and on what. Set monthly goals and stay accountable.

**Collection building** — If you're collecting a set of dishes or vintage toys, the Loot Log shows what you have and what you're still hunting for.

**Resale tracking** — Bought it for $20, sold it for $85. The app does the math for you. Perfect if you flip finds for profit.

**Tax records** — If you sell items, keep the Loot Log for your records. It's a clean receipt.

**Nostalgia** — Scroll through and remember the hunts, the finds, the steals you scored.

## Common questions

**Q: Does my Loot Log include items I paid for in cash at a sale?**
A: Only if you log them manually. Items paid through FindA.Sale (pay requests, auctions) are automatic. For cash purchases, you can add them yourself by tapping "Add Purchase."

**Q: Can I export my Loot Log?**
A: Not yet, but you can screenshot your monthly summary or email a report to yourself.

**Q: Can I share my Loot Log with someone?**
A: Not the whole log, but you can screenshot individual items or your total stats and share them.

**Q: Does my Loot Log show my resale prices?**
A: Only if you mark an item as sold and enter the price. That information is private to you.

**Q: Can I delete items from my Loot Log?**
A: Yes. Tap an item and select "Remove from Loot Log." It disappears from your history (but you can't recover it after deletion).

**Q: Is my Loot Log visible to other shoppers?**
A: No. It's completely private. Other shoppers can see your public profile and feedback, but not your purchases.

**Q: What if I bought something at a sale but don't remember the price?**
A: Check your receipts or messages with the organizer. If it was a cash transaction and you can't recall, just estimate. The Loot Log is for your reference, not tax audits (unless you're running a resale business).

**Q: Can I mark something as "traded" instead of sold?**
A: Not directly in the Loot Log, but when you complete a trade, the traded item is noted in your Trades history separately.

**Q: How far back does the Loot Log go?**
A: All the way to your first purchase. You can scroll back years.

**Q: Can I add notes about shipping or condition issues?**
A: Yes. Tap an item, scroll down to "Notes," and add whatever you want. It's your inventory.

## Tips

- **Log immediately after buying** — Don't wait. Fresh data is easier to remember accurately.
- **Add notes about condition** — If an item had a hidden flaw or needed repair, note it. Useful if you're tracking quality from a specific organizer.
- **Track your monthly average** — The Loot Log shows stats. Use it to set a budget and see if you're staying on track.
- **Check before you buy again** — Before placing a hold on an item, search your Loot Log. You might already own one.
