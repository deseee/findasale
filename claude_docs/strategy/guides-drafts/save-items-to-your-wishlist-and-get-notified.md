# Save Items to Your Wishlist and Get Notified

Stop scrolling the same yard sales every weekend hoping that dresser hasn't sold yet. Save items to your wishlist and we'll notify you the moment something changes.

## How it works

1. **Browse a sale** — Tap into a listing and scroll through items.
2. **Heart an item** — Tap the heart icon on any item card. It turns red and the item joins your wishlist.
3. **See your wishlist** — Tap the heart icon in the bottom navigation (or your profile > Wishlist). All your saved items in one place.
4. **Get notified when the sale starts** — If you saved an item from an upcoming sale, you'll get a notification when the sale opens.
5. **Get notified if the price drops** — Organizers sometimes adjust prices. We'll notify you if a price goes down.
6. **Get notified when it's about to sell** — If an item gets a hold or gets marked sold, we'll notify you so you know to move fast.
7. **Remove from wishlist** — Heart it again to unsave. It disappears from your list.

## Common questions

**Q: Will the seller know I saved their item?**
A: No. Your wishlist is private. The organizer only knows if you place a hold or message them directly.

**Q: When will I get notified?**
A: You get a push notification on your phone (if you allowed it) and an in-app notification. You can change your notification preferences in Settings > Notifications.

**Q: Can I organize my wishlist?**
A: Yes. You can sort by sale date, price (low to high or high to low), or when you saved it. You can also search by item name.

**Q: What if an item sells before I see the notification?**
A: Once an item is sold, it stays on your wishlist but shows as "Sold." You can learn what to look for next time (color, style, brand) and keep hunting.

**Q: Can I share my wishlist with a friend?**
A: Not directly, but you can open an item and share the link via text, email, or social media. Your friend can see that specific item and add it to their own wishlist.

**Q: Do I need an account to use the wishlist?**
A: Yes. Create a quick account with email or social login, and your wishlist syncs across all your devices.
