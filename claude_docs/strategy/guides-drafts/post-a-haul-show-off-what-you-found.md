# Post a Haul: Show Off What You Found

A haul is a photo post of the items you bought at a sale. It's how you share your finds with the FindA.Sale community, earn XP, get feedback, and connect with other shoppers.

## What is a haul?

A haul is one or more photos of items you purchased, plus a caption describing what you found and why you loved it. That's it. You can post:

- A single item that's special (a vintage piece, a rare find, something unusual)
- A pile of items from one sale or outing
- A series of photos showing each item
- Close-ups of condition, details, or maker's marks

Hauls are casual — they're not professional product photos. They're your genuine finds.

## Why post a haul?

**You earn 50 XP per haul.** That's a solid chunk of weekly XP for something you were going to take a photo of anyway.

**Other shoppers see what's out there.** Your haul post shows other hunters what deals exist at certain sales or in your area. You help them decide where to go.

**You build credibility.** Shoppers with lots of haul posts and high engagement are seen as experienced hunters. Your posts count toward achievements and Hall of Fame eligibility.

**You get feedback and tips.** Other shoppers might comment with restoration tips, pricing info, or their own finds from the same sale.

**You connect with your crew.** If you're in a crew, your hauls are highlighted in the crew feed. Crew members comment and celebrate your finds.

## How to post a haul

**From a sale detail page:**
After you buy something, the app asks "Post a haul?" A quick button tap takes you to the haul composer.

**From your profile:**
Tap the "+" icon next to your haul collection and select "New haul post."

**From the community feed:**
Tap "Compose" and select "Haul post."

Once you're in the composer:

1. **Add photos.** Take new photos or select from your camera roll. You can add up to 9 photos per haul. Arrange them in the order you want.

2. **Write a caption.** Describe what you bought, where, and why it's cool. Keep it real. Examples:
   - "Found this gorgeous oak dresser at an estate sale for $45. Needs refinishing but the dovetail drawers are solid."
   - "Yard sale haul: Pyrex bowls, vintage kitchen gadgets, and a turntable that actually works. Total spent: $28."
   - "Auctions are unpredictable. Grabbed this set of Mid-century dining chairs for $120. They clean up beautifully."

3. **Tag your sale (optional).** If the app knows which sale you visited, it'll suggest the tag. Tagging helps other shoppers find posts from specific sales.

4. **Tag your crew (optional).** If you're in a crew, you can tag them so they see it in the crew feed first.

5. **Post.** Hit "Share" and your haul goes live. You earn the XP immediately.

## What makes a good haul post

**Clear photos.** Good lighting. You can see the items clearly. If it's a treasure pile, a wide shot works. If it's a single piece, close-ups of details and condition matter.

**Honest caption.** Don't oversell. "Found this 1950s sideboard at a flea market for $75. Has minor veneer damage on the left side but the inside is clean" beats "STUNNING PIECE YOU WONT BELIEVE THE PRICE."

**Context.** Where did you find it? How much did you spend? What will you do with it? Other shoppers eat this stuff up.

**Engagement.** Once you post, check back. Reply to comments. If someone asks a question, answer it. High-engagement posts get boosted in feeds.

## Common mistakes to avoid

**Don't post blurry photos.** If you can't see what you bought, the haul post won't help anyone.

**Don't post items you didn't actually buy.** Haul posts are for real purchases. Wishlist items or things you saw but passed on don't count.

**Don't be vague.** "Got some stuff" is boring. Tell the story. "$15 for all of this at a Saturday yard sale in my neighborhood" is better.

**Don't post the same items twice.** If you posted something last week, don't repost it this week. The algorithm catches duplicates.

## Haul streaks and challenges

Some months FindA.Sale runs haul challenges: "Post a haul every day for a week" or "Post hauls from 5 different sale types." Completing a challenge earns bonus XP or coupons.

If you post regularly, you can build a haul streak. Streaks reset if you miss a day but rebuild quickly.

## Hall of Fame opportunities

The best haul posts get featured in the monthly Hall of Fame. Featured hauls get:
- 200 XP bonus
- A "Featured" badge on your profile
- Boosted visibility (more shoppers see your post)
- Bragging rights

Posts that get featured usually have:
- High-quality photos
- Interesting or rare finds
- Engaging captions that tell a story
- Strong community engagement (lots of likes and comments)

## Privacy and your posts

Your haul posts are public by default. Other shoppers can like, comment, and share them. If you post from a sale, the sale organizer can see your haul post too — many organizers use shopper posts as free advertising.

You can delete a haul post anytime from your profile.
