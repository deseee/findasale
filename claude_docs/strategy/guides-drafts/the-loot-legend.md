# The Loot Legend: Your Lifetime Spending and Finds

Your Loot Legend is a personal dashboard that tracks everything you've bought, saved, and found across every sale on FindA.Sale. It's a record of your hunting history and a way to celebrate how much value you've discovered.

## What the Loot Legend shows

Your Loot Legend displays:

**Lifetime statistics:**
- Total items found (across all sales and purchases)
- Total money spent (sum of all purchases)
- Total money saved (estimated value of items vs. retail price)
- Favorite sale types (which sales you hunt most: yard sales, auctions, estate sales, flea markets)
- Most-visited cities and states
- Longest streak (your best consecutive login streak ever)

**Personal records:**
- Biggest haul (most expensive single purchase)
- Best bargain (highest estimated retail value vs. lowest price paid)
- Most items in one day (shopping spree record)
- Favorite organizer (you've bought from them the most)

**Achievements and badges:**
- All badges you've unlocked
- Current rank
- Hall of Fame features (if any)
- Total XP earned (lifetime and this month)

## Viewing your Loot Legend

Your Loot Legend is on your profile under the "Stats" tab. You can see it anytime, and other shoppers can view your public Loot Legend too (they'll see your rank, badges, favorite cities, and lifetime totals, but not your spending amount).

## What the stats tell you

Your Loot Legend is partly for fun — it's satisfying to see how many items you've found and how much you've saved. But it also reveals your hunting patterns:

- If your biggest saves come from estate sales, you know to prioritize those
- If flea markets are your favorite, you're building a hunting routine
- If your best bargains are in one city, you might focus hunting there

Some shoppers use their Loot Legend to set goals: "This month I want to find 25 items" or "I want to hit $500 in total savings."

## Saving estimates

The Loot Legend estimates how much money you've saved by comparing your purchase price to the estimated retail value of each item. These estimates are based on:

- Comparable listings on the web
- Recent sales of similar items
- Organizer condition notes
- Your haul post descriptions

The estimate isn't a guarantee of actual retail value — it's a rough guide. But if you bought a lamp for $3 and similar lamps sell for $40 elsewhere, your Loot Legend marks that as a $37 save.

## Tracking over time

Your Loot Legend updates automatically. Every time you buy something, post a haul, or unlock an achievement, your stats refresh.

You can compare your stats month-to-month to see if you're hunting more or less, if your spending patterns are changing, or if you're hitting your personal goals.

## Privacy notes

Your Loot Legend is public by default — other shoppers can see your rank, badges, and lifetime totals. You can toggle your profile to private if you want to hide your stats from other shoppers. Even on private, you'll still see your full Loot Legend yourself.

Some shoppers make their profile private to avoid being recruited into crews or bounties too aggressively.
