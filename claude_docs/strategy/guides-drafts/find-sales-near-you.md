# Find Sales Near You: Map, Search, and Calendar

Looking for a yard sale, auction, or consignment haul this weekend? FindA.Sale shows you everything happening within your radius—then filters by what you actually want.

## How it works

1. **Grant location access** — The app asks for your location once (on web or mobile). You can change it anytime.
2. **See the map** — Sales appear as pins. Tap a pin to see the name, address, hours, and opening date.
3. **Switch to list view** — Prefer a text list? Tap the list icon. Sort by distance, rating, or start date.
4. **Use the calendar** — Tap "Calendar" to see all sales happening on a specific date. Great for planning a weekend route.
5. **Filter by type** — Tap "Type" and check the boxes: Yard Sales, Estate Sales, Auctions, Flea Markets, Consignment. Only see what you hunt.
6. **Filter by distance** — Slide the radius up or down. Default is 25 miles—change it to 10 or 50.
7. **Spot featured sales** — Sales with a star badge ⭐ are highlighted by organizers. They often have more inventory or special categories.
8. **Tap a sale** — See the full details: address, hours, categories, organizer info, and shopper reviews.

## Common questions

**Q: Can I save a sale to check later?**
A: Yes. Open the sale detail and tap the heart icon to save it. Your saved sales appear on your profile under "My Sales."

**Q: What does the star badge mean?**
A: That sale is featured. Organizers choose to highlight certain sales, usually because they have a large inventory, rare finds, or a special theme. It doesn't mean it's better—just more visible.

**Q: How far does "near you" search?**
A: You set it. Default is 25 miles, but you can drag the distance slider to 5, 10, 50, or 100+ miles.

**Q: Why is a sale showing on the map but not starting until next weekend?**
A: Organizers post sales before they start. You can still see the listing, read the description, and save it. Once the sale opens, you'll get a notification if you followed the organizer.

**Q: I'm traveling. Can I change my location?**
A: Tap the location icon at the top and type a city name or zip code. The map recenters and shows sales near that spot.

**Q: Can I filter by price?**
A: Not directly on the map. But on the sale detail page, you can filter items by price range using the search box.
