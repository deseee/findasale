# Browse by City: Weekend Sale Pages

Planning a road trip or want to see what's happening in the next town over? City pages show you every sale starting that weekend, sorted by category and distance. Great for building a multi-stop Saturday.

## How it works

1. **Navigate to Cities** — Tap "Cities" from the home menu or search bar.
2. **Pick a city** — Search by name (Chicago, Austin, Denver) or scroll your state's popular cities. Tap one to see the weekend view.
3. **See all sales for that weekend** — The page shows every sale starting Friday, Saturday, or Sunday. Sales are grouped by distance from the city center.
4. **Scan by category** — Scroll down to see different sale types: Yard Sales, Estate Sales, Auctions, Flea Markets, Consignment Shops.
5. **Check the sidebar** — On the right, you'll see how many sales of each type, plus the total count.
6. **Tap a sale** — See the full listing, hours, categories, and shopper reviews.
7. **Bookmark the city** — Tap the star icon to bookmark this city page. It saves to your profile so you can check it every weekend.

## Why use city pages instead of the map

- **Plan a route fast** — Instead of pinning individual sales on a map, you see everything at once and build a logical route.
- **Discover outside your usual radius** — Your map defaults to 25 miles. City pages show you what's happening 30, 50, or 100+ miles away without resetting your location.
- **See the full weekend** — The page shows all sales starting that Saturday and Sunday, so you can plan accordingly.
- **Check regularly** — Bookmark a city you visit often. Each week the list updates with new sales.

## Common questions

**Q: How far from the city center do the sales show?**
A: The page shows sales within roughly 50 miles of the city center. You can tap a sale and see its exact address and distance.

**Q: Can I filter the city page by sale type?**
A: Not yet, but you can scan the categories listed below. Tap a sale type heading to jump to those listings.

**Q: Are the sales on the city page ranked by something?**
A: They're sorted by distance from the city center, closest first. Within each distance band, they're sorted by organizer rating.

**Q: Can I set alerts for a specific city?**
A: Not directly, but you can bookmark the city and check it each weekend. You'll also see new sales from that city if you follow specific organizers there.

**Q: What if I want to see sales from a weekday, not just the weekend?**
A: City pages focus on the upcoming weekend by default. To see weekday sales, use the map or search by date.

**Q: Can I export the city's sales to my calendar?**
A: Not the whole city, but you can save individual sales to your calendar through each sale's detail page.
