# How Disputes and Refunds Work

Disputes happen when a shopper and organizer disagree about a transaction. FindA.Sale has a structured process to resolve disputes fairly.

## When to open a dispute

Open a dispute if:

- **Item doesn't match description.** You bought a "Good" condition chair and it arrived with major damage not mentioned.
- **Item didn't arrive.** You paid for something and the organizer didn't deliver.
- **Wrong item was sent.** You ordered a blue lamp, received a red one.
- **Significant misrepresentation.** Photos showed one thing; reality was different.
- **Price disagreement.** You agreed on a price and they charged something else.

**Note:** Minor flaws, different personal taste, or "I changed my mind" aren't valid dispute reasons.

## How to open a dispute

**From your purchase history:**
1. Find the transaction
2. Tap "Open dispute"
3. Choose the reason
4. Upload photos/evidence (if applicable)
5. Write a detailed explanation
6. Submit

The organizer is immediately notified.

## The dispute timeline

**Review period: 72 hours**
Both the shopper and organizer have 72 hours to provide evidence, respond, and try to resolve it together.

**FindA.Sale review: 5–7 business days**
If you don't resolve it yourselves, FindA.Sale steps in. We review the evidence:
- Purchase history
- Photos from the listing
- Messages between you
- Condition notes from the organizer
- Photos you submitted

**Decision: Final**
Once FindA.Sale makes a decision, it's final. No appeals.

## Possible outcomes

**Shopper refund (full or partial).** The organizer refunds your money.

**No refund.** The description matched, the organizer fulfilled their obligation, and your complaint isn't valid.

**Replace the item.** The organizer sends you a replacement (rare, usually only for "wrong item" disputes).

**Split decision.** You both shared responsibility. The organizer refunds 50%.

## Evidence that helps

**Photographs.** Clear photos of the item showing damage or discrepancies with the description.

**Message history.** Screenshots of conversations where you asked about condition and the organizer made claims.

**Listing screenshots.** Photos of the original listing to prove what was promised.

**Shipping proof.** If mailed, tracking numbers showing delivery.

**Witness.** If someone was with you, their account helps (though FindA.Sale prioritizes objective evidence).

## What doesn't help

- Complaints about personal taste ("I don't like the color")
- Vague claims without evidence ("It was damaged")
- Threats or hostile language (disqualifies your case)
- Claims about items you never received (without shipping proof)

## Refund processing

If you win a dispute:

**Full refund:** Returned to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

**Partial refund:** Same timeline, prorated amount.

**What about the item?**
- If it's a small item (under $20), you usually keep it.
- If it's large/expensive, the organizer might request return shipping.
- Organizer covers return shipping for cases where they were at fault.

## Organizer perspective

Organizers want to resolve disputes quickly too. Most disputes are resolved in the first 72 hours because:
- A refund is cheaper than a lengthy dispute
- Reputation damage from disputes is significant
- Many disputes are genuine misunderstandings (fixable with good communication)

If an organizer ignores your dispute or is uncooperative, FindA.Sale sides with the shopper.

## Patterns of disputes

If an organizer gets 3+ disputes in a month, FindA.Sale flags them. Repeated disputes result in warnings, reduced listing privileges, or removal from the platform.

If a shopper opens 5+ disputes in a month, we check if they're serial abusers. Serial disputers face account restrictions.

## Preventing disputes in the first place

**For shoppers:**
- Ask detailed questions before buying
- Request photos if the listing ones aren't clear
- Read condition notes carefully
- Buy from high-reputation organizers

**For organizers:**
- Be brutally honest in descriptions
- Photograph damage and wear
- Respond to questions quickly and truthfully
- Disclose all flaws upfront

Prevention is better than disputes.

## Dispute fees

There are no dispute fees for either party. FindA.Sale absorbs the cost of dispute resolution.

## International disputes

If you're buying from or selling to someone in a different country, disputes become complex (shipping, customs, currency, jurisdiction). FindA.Sale supports these but resolution takes longer.

## Escalation

If you believe FindA.Sale made an unfair decision, you can request a review. Include new evidence or context we missed. We'll re-examine, but this rarely changes the outcome.
