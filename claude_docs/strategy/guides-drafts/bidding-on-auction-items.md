# Bidding on Auction Items

Auctions let you bid against other shoppers in real-time or set a proxy bid and let the system bid for you. Here's how bidding works on FindA.Sale.

## How it works

1. **Find an auction item** — Browse a sale marked as an auction and tap an item to see the current bid and time remaining.
2. **Place a bid** — Tap the bid field and enter your amount. It must be higher than the current bid plus the minimum increment (usually $0.25–$1.00).
3. **Confirm** — Tap "Place Bid" and the system records your bid. If you're the highest bidder, your name appears (unless you bid anonymously).
4. **Get outbid** — If someone bids higher, you'll get a notification. You can place a new bid or let it go.
5. **Proxy bidding** — Instead of watching in real-time, set your maximum bid and the system bids for you automatically. It raises your bid only as high as needed to stay the highest, up to your max.
6. **Win or lose** — When the auction ends, the highest bidder wins. You'll get a notification to pay and pick up the item.

## Bidding strategies

**Bid early** — Place a bid early to signal interest. Early bids don't guarantee a win but show you're serious.

**Use proxy bidding** — Set your max bid and let the system work. You won't overpay emotionally.

**Bid strategically** — Don't bid every time you're outbid. Set a max and stick to it.

**Watch the clock** — Auctions often heat up in the last minutes. If time is running out and you really want it, bid now.

## Common questions

**Q: Can I bid anonymously?**
A: Yes. When you place a bid, you can choose to bid as "Anonymous Bidder" instead of showing your name.

**Q: What's the minimum bid increase?**
A: It varies by auction and starting price, but typically $0.25 on small items and $1–$5 on larger ones. The app shows the minimum for each item.

**Q: Can I lower my maximum proxy bid?**
A: No. Once you set a proxy bid, you can't reduce it. You can place a new, higher bid, but the max stays where you set it.

**Q: What if the auction extends past my bedtime?**
A: Use proxy bidding. You set your max and go to sleep. The system keeps bidding if needed.

**Q: Do I pay right away if I win?**
A: Not immediately, but you'll get a notification to pay within 24 hours. You can pay online or arrange a time to pick up and pay in cash.

**Q: Can I retract a bid?**
A: Generally no, but if you bid by accident or have an emergency, message the organizer right away. They might let you retract if it's within a few minutes.

**Q: What if two people bid the exact same amount?**
A: The first bid placed wins.

**Q: Are there any bid limits I should know about?**
A: No price cap, but use common sense. Don't bid more than you're willing to pay.
