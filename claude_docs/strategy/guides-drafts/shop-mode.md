Shop Mode keeps your storefront open all the time.
No start or end date, no manual republishing — your items stay live and searchable until you mark them sold or remove them.
Shop Mode is a TEAMS-tier feature.

---

## What Shop Mode is

A standard sale has a start date and an end date.
Shop Mode removes that window.

Your storefront is always open.
New items go live the moment you publish them.
Nothing expires on a close date.

Instead of a countdown timer, your listing shows an **Always Open** badge.
Shoppers know they can check back anytime — there's no rush.

---

## Who it's for

Shop Mode is designed for organizers who run ongoing inventory rather than one-time events:

- Consignment shops with rotating stock
- Estate liquidators who take in new inventory continuously
- Flea market regulars with a permanent booth
- Organizers transitioning from one-off sales to a steady storefront

If you run occasional yard sales or auctions, a standard sale listing is the right tool.
Shop Mode is for organizers who are always selling.

---

## Requirements

Shop Mode requires the **TEAMS tier**.
If your account is on a lower tier, the Shop Mode toggle will appear grayed out in Settings.
Upgrade to TEAMS to enable it.

---

## How to enable Shop Mode

1. Go to **Settings** from your organizer dashboard.
2. Find the **Shop Mode** section.
3. Toggle **Shop Mode** on.
4. Set your **auto-renew interval**: 7, 14, or 30 days.
5. Tap **Save**.

Your storefront is now in Shop Mode.

---

## What auto-renewing means

Shop Mode refreshes your storefront automatically on the interval you set.

A 7-day interval means the sale renews every 7 days.
A 30-day interval means it renews monthly.

Renewing moves your storefront to the top of recently-updated listings in search.
It also resets the "last updated" timestamp shoppers see on your profile.

You don't have to do anything for the renewal to happen.
There's no email, no button to click — it runs on its own.

---

## How inventory works in Shop Mode

Inventory in Shop Mode works the same as a regular sale.

- Add an item → it's live immediately
- Edit an item → changes appear right away
- Mark an item sold → it leaves the active listing
- Delete an item → it's removed entirely

Items don't expire based on a close date.
If you stop adding new inventory, old items stay listed until you remove them or mark them sold.

---

## What's different from a standard sale

| | Standard sale | Shop Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Start/end dates | Required | None |
| Countdown timer on item cards | Yes | No |
| Listing badge | "Starts [date]" or "Active Now" | "Always Open" |
| Items expire on close date | Yes | No |
| Manual republish needed | Yes, after close | No |
| Appears in sale-discovery search | Yes | Yes |
| Auto-renew | No | Yes (7, 14, or 30 days) |

## Common questions

**Can I still run timed sales alongside Shop Mode?**
Yes. Shop Mode is a setting on your storefront. You can still create separate timed sales for specific events — an estate liquidation, an auction, a weekend pop-up. Those run independently alongside your always-open storefront.

**What happens to my existing items if I turn Shop Mode on?**
All your currently-listed items remain live. Nothing is removed or reset when you enable Shop Mode. The change is to how your storefront behaves going forward.

**Can I turn Shop Mode off?**
Yes. Go to Settings → Shop Mode and toggle it off. Your storefront reverts to standard sale behavior and you'll need to set a new start and end date. Existing items are not affected.

**Does Shop Mode cost extra on top of TEAMS?**
No. Shop Mode is included in the TEAMS tier — no additional charge.

**I'm on PRO. Can I use Shop Mode?**
No. Shop Mode is TEAMS-only. Upgrade your account to TEAMS to enable it.

**Will shoppers know my store is in Shop Mode?**
They'll see the "Always Open" badge on your storefront instead of a countdown or date range. They won't see the words "Shop Mode" — just the badge.
