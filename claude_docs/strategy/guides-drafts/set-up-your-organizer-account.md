# Set Up Your Organizer Account

Get your organizer account running in five minutes. You'll create your login, verify your email, and be ready to post your first sale.

## How it works

1. Go to the registration page and enter your email address and password.
2. Check your email for a verification link and click it to confirm your address.
3. Enter your organizer name (the name that appears on your sale listings).
4. Choose your plan (you can upgrade anytime).
5. You're in. Your dashboard is ready.

## Common questions

**Q: Can I change my organizer name later?**
A: Yes. Go to Settings → Account and update it whenever you need. Changes take effect immediately on new listings.

**Q: Do I have to pick a plan right now?**
A: You start with Simple automatically. You can upgrade to Pro or Teams later from Settings → Plan, or skip it and upgrade when you post your second sale.

**Q: What email should I use?**
A: Use an email you check regularly. This is where we send payout receipts, sale alerts, and password reset links.

**Q: Can two people run one organizer account?**
A: Yes, but they'll share the same login. For separate access, add them as staff members from Settings → Staff. That way they can manage sales but can't change payouts or billing.

**Q: I forgot my password.**
A: Go to the login page and click "Forgot password?" We'll send a reset link to your email.

**Q: How do I use the same account for multiple sale locations?**
A: Create a sale, then add as many locations (hubs) as you need. All locations post under your organizer name, but shoppers see the address for each hub.

## Screen capture script

Open a web browser and navigate to the FindA.Sale registration page. Type in a test email address in the email field. Notice the password requirements shown below the field. Enter a password that meets those requirements. Tap the "Create account" button. Check your email inbox and click the verification link. You're now on the account details page. Enter an organizer name — this is what shoppers will see on your sale listings. See the plan selection section below. Notice Simple is selected by default. Click "Finish setup" at the bottom. Your dashboard appears with a welcome message and empty sales list. Notice the Settings link in the navigation — that's where you'll add staff, change your name, or upgrade your plan later.
