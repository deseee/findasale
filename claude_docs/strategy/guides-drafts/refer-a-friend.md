When you refer a friend to FindA.Sale and they make their first purchase, you both get something. This page covers the basics. The live reward amounts and your personal referral link are on [your referrals page](/shopper/referrals).

---

## How it works

**Get your link.** Go to [/shopper/referrals](/shopper/referrals). Your personal referral link is at the top. Copy it and share it however you want — text, social media, wherever.

**Your friend signs up.** When they click your link and create an account, they are connected to you.

**Your friend makes their first purchase.** Once they complete a purchase on FindA.Sale, the referral locks in for both of you.

**You both get rewarded.** Your friend gets a first-purchase bonus. You get XP credited to your account plus a coupon toward your next FindA.Sale transaction.

## Track your referrals

Your [referrals dashboard](/shopper/referrals) shows everyone you have referred, whether they have made a purchase yet, and the rewards you have earned. Rewards that are pending (friend signed up but has not purchased yet) show separately from rewards that are ready to use.

## Current reward amounts

Reward amounts update periodically. The current amounts are always shown on your [referrals page](/shopper/referrals) — that page is the source of truth, not this guide.

---

## Common questions

**Does my friend need to use my link when they sign up?**
Yes. If they sign up directly without your link, the referral will not connect. Send them your personal link from [/shopper/referrals](/shopper/referrals).

**Is there a limit to how many friends I can refer?**
No cap. Every completed referral earns rewards.

**How long until I see the coupon in my account?**
Rewards post within 24 hours of your friend's first completed purchase.
