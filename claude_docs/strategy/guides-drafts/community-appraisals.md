When you can't find a comparable sale price for an unusual item, the community appraisal tool lets you ask experienced collectors for a valuation opinion. You get a response within 24–48 hours.

It's most useful for items where online comparables are hard to find: vintage jewelry, signed memorabilia, regional art, unusual antiques, estate pieces with no maker's mark.

**To submit an item for appraisal:**
1. Open the item in your inventory.
2. Tap **Request Appraisal**.
3. Confirm the item details are accurate — clear photo, accurate description.
4. Tap **Submit**.

Experienced collectors in the FindA.Sale community review it and submit their valuations. You'll see the responses in your appraisals dashboard at **/organizer/appraisals**.

**Cost:** Free for PRO and TEAMS organizers. Uses XP for SIMPLE tier.

For details on how appraisers evaluate items and how to interpret the responses they give — including what to do when you get a wide range of opinions — see the [full community appraisals guide](/guide#community-appraisals).
