# Trade Items With Other Shoppers

Have a dining chair you don't love? See a lamp at a consignment shop you do? Skip the cash and propose a trade. Both of you get what you want without paying.

## How it works

1. **Open Trades** — Tap your profile > Trades, or navigate to the Trades section.
2. **See available items** — You'll see items from other shoppers that are listed as "available for trade." Browse by category or search.
3. **Propose a trade** — Tap an item and propose what you're willing to trade for it. You can upload a photo of your item (existing sale inventory or something from your home).
4. **Wait for response** — The other shopper gets a notification and can accept, decline, or counter-offer (suggest a different item of yours).
5. **Agree on details** — Once both agree, message each other to set a pickup time and location (your home, a sale location, or a safe public spot).
6. **Exchange** — Meet up and swap. No money changes hands, just items.
7. **Mark complete** — Once you've both received your items, mark the trade as complete in the app.

## What you can trade

You can trade items from:
- **Your active sale listings** — If you're an organizer selling items, shoppers can propose trades.
- **Items you've bought** — If you bought something at a sale and want to get rid of it, add it to your trade inventory.
- **Items from home** — Anything you own. Take a photo and upload it.

## Tips for successful trades

**Be clear about condition** — Photos should show any wear, damage, or cosmetic issues. The more honest you are, the fewer disputes later.

**Suggest a fair swap** — A $50 item for a $50 item is fair. A $100 item for a $10 item usually gets declined.

**Pick a safe meeting spot** — A sale location, a public parking lot, or daytime at home. Trust your gut.

**Communicate fast** — Don't ghost. If you're no longer interested, say so. If you want to counter-offer, respond within 24 hours.

**Leave feedback** — After the trade, leave a comment about the other person. Were they on time? Was the item as described? It builds trust.

## Common questions

**Q: What if the trade falls through at the last minute?**
A: Message the other person and let them know. If you had agreed on a specific time and location, a heads-up matters. You can report the user if they consistently flake, but most people are reasonable.

**Q: Do I have to trade in person?**
A: Yes, currently trades are in-person only. The app doesn't handle shipping. You arrange a time and place with the other shopper.

**Q: What if I want to back out of a trade?**
A: If the other person hasn't accepted yet, you can cancel. If they accepted and you want to back out, message them—they might understand, but it could hurt your reputation.

**Q: Can I trade with someone outside my area?**
A: Not easily, since trades are in-person. You'd have to arrange shipping on your own, which defeats the purpose. Stick to local trades.

**Q: What if I trade something and it breaks within a week?**
A: That's why photos matter. If you traded something misrepresented, reach out to the other person. Most will make it right. If not, you can report the issue to FindA.Sale support.

**Q: Can I trade multiple items for one item?**
A: Yes. Propose a bundle: "I'll trade you my chair, side table, and lamp for your dresser." The other person can accept, decline, or counter.

**Q: Is there a time limit on trades?**
A: You can propose a trade whenever, but both people have 7 days to arrange a meetup. If neither confirms a time by day 7, the trade expires.

**Q: Can I see other shoppers' trade history?**
A: You can see their feedback and how many successful trades they've completed, but not the specific items.
