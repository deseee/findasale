# Spike 1: Real-Operator Seeding — 2-4 Week Sprint

**Executive Summary**

FindA.Sale currently has 1 real operator (Patrick's family sale) plus seed/fake data. This spike is the ground game to acquire 5–10 real organizers running real sales with real data before any broader campaign. Three parallel tracks (Concierge, Founding-Operator cash incentives, Hyper-Local hand-recruit) executed over 2–4 weeks, backed by accelerated real-data population via family sale retroentry and eBay sync. Goal: by mid-May, the platform displays real sales, real items, real transactions, and real organizer testimonials. This gates Spike 2 (viral content + influencer outreach) and Spike 3 (paid acquisition).

---

## Track A: Concierge Onboarding Offer

**What It Is**

Patrick personally onboards the first 10 organizers. Each gets:
- 1 kickoff call (30 min): walkthrough of platform, organizer expectations, what Patrick will do
- Daily Slack check-in (5–10 min async) for first 5 days post-launch
- One in-person (or Zoom if remote) sales observation (2–4 hours on sale day)
- Post-sale debrief call (30 min)
- Weekly check-in for 4 weeks (15 min sync)

**What Success Looks Like**
- Organizer ships their first sale on FindA.Sale
- Real items (not seed data), real pricing, real shopper engagement (holds, purchases, or just saves)
- Organizer completes sale and marks items sold
- Organizer provides testimonial + one photo from the sale (signed consent)

**What Patrick Stops Doing After This**
- Patrick does NOT become an ongoing business partner or sale operator
- Patrick does NOT troubleshoot technical issues (redirects to in-app help + FAQ)
- After week 4 check-in, organizer is independent or churns out (expected some churn)

**Time Budget for Patrick**
- Per organizer: ~8 hours total (1x kickoff + daily 5min × 5 + 2–4h observation + 0.5h debrief + 4× weekly 15min check-ins)
- 10 organizers × 8 hours = 80 hours over 4 weeks ≈ 2 hours per business day
- Feasible if scheduled: Mon/Wed/Fri concierge slots, Tue/Thu focus on other tracks

**Acceptance Criteria**
- [ ] At least 7 of 10 organizers ship their first sale on FindA.Sale (70% conversion)
- [ ] 5 of 10 provide testimonial + photo + signed consent (50% content harvest)
- [ ] Real-data samples collected (screenshot items, pricing, hold activity)

## Track B: Founding-Operator Program (Cash Incentive)

**Offer Structure**

Pay first 10 organizers a flat incentive in exchange for testimonial + case study + name/likeness consent.

**Two Options (Patrick Chooses One Before Launch)**

**Option B1: Cash ($750 per organizer)**
- Total cost: $7,500 (10 × $750)
- Timing: $375 upfront (after first sale ships), $375 on completion (case study delivered + consent signed)
- Why this works: Organizers get real money fast, incentivizes action

**Option B2: Free PRO for Life (enterprise-value: $29/mo = $3,480/year)**
- Total cost: 10 years of $29/mo × 10 = ~$34,800 (but actually $29/mo per user, annually capped)
- Realistic cost: $350/mo recurring, ~$4,200/year, ~$21k over 6 years
- Why this works: No upfront cash burn, long-term platform evangelism, shows confidence in product

**Option B3: Hybrid ($300 cash + 3 months free PRO)**
- Total cost: $3,000 + $87/organizer in value = ~$3,870
- Sweetest middle ground if cash is tight

**Recommendation: Option B1 ($750 cash)** — Fast, clear incentive, low future liability. If strapped, Option B3 hybrid.

**Deliverables Required from Each Organizer**
1. Testimonial quote (3–5 sentences, scripted with options or free-form)
2. Case study outline (10 min call, Patrick ghost-writes from notes)
3. One sale photo (organizer or sale detail, high-quality)
4. Name + likeness consent (one-page doc, Patrick provides template)

**Operator Agreement Template Outline**
- Scope: Founding operator designation, $750 incentive (or equivalent), case study collaboration, consent
- Duration: 6 months (case study created by end of Month 2)
- IP: Patrick owns case study content; organizer retains anonymity option
- No exclusivity, no non-compete
- One line: "Organizer agrees to allow FindA.Sale to use their name, sale title, and provided photos in case studies and marketing"

**Patrick Legal Requirement** 
Get one-page agreement template reviewed by an attorney before rolling out (estimated $300–500). Do not proceed without it.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- [ ] 8 of 10 organizers cash out + deliver testimonial + consent
- [ ] 6 of 10 complete full case study (name/photo/quote)
- [ ] 3–5 case studies published on website + social before May 31

## Track C: Hyper-Local Hand-Recruit (Saturday Mornings)

**The Tactic**

Patrick walks the Saturday estate-sale circuit in Grand Rapids for 4 consecutive Saturdays (May 4, 11, 18, 25). Target: sign 10 organizers on-the-spot or within 48 hours of contact.

**Pre-Walk Research (1–2 hours per Saturday)**

Source estate sales running that weekend from:
- `finda.sale` own listings (daily update)
- EstateSales.NET (free, searchable by zip + date)
- EstateSales.org (free listings)
- Craigslist (GR + surrounding counties, "estate sale" keyword)
- Facebook Marketplace (GR groups, this week posts)

Create a **Saturday Prospect List** (Google Sheet)
- Sale address, organizer name (if public), sale date/hours, estimated size (house type)
- Drive time from Patrick's location
- Sort by geographic cluster (reduce drive time)
- Target 4–6 sales per Saturday within 20-min radius of GR downtown

**In-Person Pitch (60 seconds, elevator version)**

**Goal:** Get a business card + QR code scan + one-minute demo on phone

**Script (adapt to energy of conversation):**
"Hi, I'm Patrick, I built an app for sale organizers. It solves the problem of [showcasing items to shoppers before Saturday / getting real-time holds and sales data]. Takes 5 minutes to set up, keeps 10% commission—same as other platforms. [Show quick demo: pull up a sample sale on phone, show the item listing, the shopper view, one hold screenshot]. No contract, no fees unless you use it. Here's a QR code—scan it, I can get you set up within an hour if you want to give it a try. Even if not, I'd love your feedback."

**Business Card + QR Design**
- Front: FindA.Sale logo, Patrick's name + phone (if comfortable), "Real-time sale management + shopper engagement"
- Back: QR code linking to `/organizer/signup?ref=gr-saturday-hand-recruit`
- Print 50–100 cards per Saturday

**Demo Device Prep**
- iPhone with fully-charged battery + portable charger
- Pre-loaded demo account with 2–3 real (or polished seed) sales
- Cloudinary sample images of typical estate sale items
- Screenshots of holds and transactions bookmarked for quick show
- Demo login ready to go (no waiting for OTP)

**Follow-Up Cadence (critical)**
- Saturday evening: send text/email within 4 hours (short form, friendly tone, link to setup)
- Tuesday: if no signup, one phone call (try to reach organizer in evening during dinner-planning time)
- Thursday: final text, offer to jump on a quick call for questions
- Drop-off after Thursday if no response

**Conversion Expectations**
- 6 sales per Saturday × 4 weekends = 24 conversations
- Pitch-to-scan rate: 50% (12 scans)
- Scan-to-signup rate: 60% (7 signups)
- Signup-to-first-sale rate: 70% (5 first sales)
- Target: 5 organizers with real sales from Track C alone

**Geographic Radius Rule**
- Do NOT drive more than 20 min from Patrick's GR location per sale
- If a sale is 30+ min drive, skip it (time wasted > value of one new organizer)
- Focus on concentrated Saturday clusters in downtown GR, East Hills, Eastown, Forest Hills

**Acceptance Criteria**
- [ ] At least 12 business card scans over 4 Saturdays
- [ ] At least 7 signups from hand-recruit
- [ ] At least 5 first sales shipped by organizers from Track C by end of May

**Time Cost:**

## Track E: Founding-Operator Operations (Week 2–4 Cadence)

Once 5–10 real organizers are onboarded (end of Week 1 or early Week 2), establish a light weekly cadence to keep them engaged and harvest content.

**Weekly Check-In Template (async, Slack)**

Every Monday (send at 9am, expect 48-hour response window):
- "How'd your sale go this weekend? Any technical snags?" (open-ended)
- Link to mini-survey (Google Form, 2 min):
  - How many items? How many shoppers? Favorite feature?
  - One optional video testimonial prompt: "Tell us in 30 seconds how FindA.Sale helped your sale"
- Highlight: "If you refer another organizer and they sign up, you get $25 credit"

**Content-Extraction Routine**

End of each week (Friday):
1. Review shopper activity: holds, purchases, saves, reviews (track in simple spreadsheet)
2. Pull one screenshot per organizer (best-looking sale page or transaction proof)
3. Send organizer a friendly message: "Your sale looks awesome! Can I use this screenshot in a case study or social post?" (async consent)
4. Compile weekly digest: "#X organizers active, #Y holds placed, #Z items sold, $A revenue generated"

**Churn Early-Warning Signals**

If an organizer doesn't respond to two consecutive Monday check-ins:
- Wednesday: send a personal text/email: "Missing you—anything we can help with?"
- If still no response after 1 week: one phone call to understand churn reason (feedback)
- Log churn reason in spreadsheet (bugs, confusing UI, poor customer support, found competitor, didn't need it, too busy)

**Referral Program** (optional, depends on Patrick bandwidth)

Offer $25 credit (applied to account) for each organizer they refer who signs up. No complex tracking—just: "If you know another organizer, send them your signup link. You'll both get $25 off."

**Acceptance Criteria**
- [ ] 8 of 10 organizers engaged (respond to at least 2 of 4 Monday check-ins)
- [ ] 5+ content assets (screenshots, testimonials, video clips) collected
- [ ] 3+ organizers actively referred 1 new organizer (6+ secondary signups)
- [ ] Churn reasons documented (feedback log for product iteration)
