Visceral because it's **empathetic, specific, and problem-focused**—not a win story, but a relief story. Episode formula: organizer talks through unsold items, reveals the math (storage cost + relisting time + emotional drag), then walks through FindA.Sale solution.

Organizer explains: storage space (cost, rent, time spent managing), relisting effort (copy written 3x for eBay, Facebook, Mercari, local classifieds), markdown pressure (after 2 weeks, items are stale, price-drop necessary), emotional drag ("I feel like I failed the estate"), opportunity cost (time to relist = time not on next sale).
   - **Graphics overlay:** Daily storage cost ($5-20/day depending on space), hours spent relisting (estimate 3-4 hours), percentage markdown needed (15-40%), time-to-sell if re-listed (7-14 days vs. 1-2 during sale).
3. **The old way (90s):** Show organizer manually updating 4 platforms, writing descriptions, taking new photos, checking sold-not-sold status across tabs. Frustration captured.
4. **FindA.Sale solution walkthrough (120s):** Same organizer (or different organizer if Piece 3 partner) opens FindA.Sale unsold/archive section, shows: auto-markdown cycle scheduling, multi-channel publishing (one click, goes to eBay + Mercari + Facebook simultaneously), hold/release toggles, settlement accounting for markdown items. ## Production & Brand Guardrails

### General Content Rules (Across All 5 Pieces)

1. **No "AI" in copy or voiceover.** Use "Auto," "Smart," "Suggested" instead. Decision D-006 locked.
   - ❌ "AI-powered auto-tagging"
   - ✅ "Smart auto-tagging" or "Auto tags"

2. **No "estate sale" as sole sale type.** Weave all sale types naturally:
   - ❌ "FindA.Sale is for estate sales (and also garage sales and auctions)."
   - ✅ "Estate sales, garage sales, auctions, flea markets — FindA.Sale helps organizers of all resale events."

3. **No get-rich-quick framing.** This attracts wrong cohort (flippers, speculation). Brand is about real organizers, fair pricing, sustainable resale.
   - ❌ "Flip items for profit. Easy money from FindA.Sale."
   - ✅ "Run efficient sales. Keep more of what you earn. Fair marketplace."

4. **Authenticity > polish.** Shaky phone footage, real organizers, unscripted moments are the vibe. Over-produced = loses trust.
   - Do: Handheld, natural light, real locations, real people
   - Don't: Studio setups, actors, stock footage, fake testimonials

5. **Respectful framing of estate sales & grief.** These are sensitive contexts. Acknowledge emotional weight, don't trivialize.
   - Don't: "Get that stuff outta here!" (flippant)
   - Do: "Managing an estate is emotional work. We're here to handle logistics so you can focus on honoring the legacy."

6. **Fair pricing / no exploitation.** Never suggest organizers should charge "just to clear inventory" or position shoppers as bargain hunters who are "stealing." Emphasize fair-value marketplace.

### Visual Tone Notes (For All Pieces)

- **Photography:** Real items, real organizers, real shoppers. Avoid stock photos. Variety of conditions (pristine, vintage, well-worn, rare, everyday).
- **Lighting:** Warm, natural when possible. Avoid harsh fluorescent or clinical lighting.
- **Color:** Leverage FindA.Sale brand palette (warm neutrals + sage green accent). Avoid oversaturation.
- **Typography on graphics:** Clear, readable. FindA.Sale brand font (check brand-voice-guide for font specs).
- **No gatekeeping aesthetic.** Include all ages, abilities, backgrounds, event types, price points. Estate sale ≠ only wealthy people.
