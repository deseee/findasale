**Date:** 2026-04-30  
**Dispatch:** findasale-innovation  
**Status:** Phase 1 (idea generation) + Phase 2 (feasibility assessment) complete

## Executive Summary

Patrick rejects founder-hustle mechanics (cold calling, in-person QR cards, daily vlogs, concierge onboarding). He needs viral mechanics where **one user's action pulls in 5+ new users with zero added founder hours**. The three highest-leverage opportunities are:

1. **Loot Drop Notification Cascade** — Platform auto-publishes weekly "Wildest Finds" reels from real sale data; each Friday → N notifications → N social shares → N new shoppers. Compounding through sale frequency, not founder effort. Ship: 2 weeks. Cost: $0 marginal (uses existing Loot Drop push + Sharpest Image rendering). Volume ceiling: 100K+ shoppers (bounded by active organizer count only).

2. **TikTok Creator Sponsorship + Auto-Attribution Loop** — Micro-sponsor 5–10 mid-tier estate-sale/antique creators ($500–2K each per month) to use the app on-camera in their existing 100K–500K-follower content. FindA.Sale auto-credits them in weekly "Creator Hauls" recap + gives them a public creator leaderboard (link traffic + status). Zero cold outreach from Patrick; creators pull their audiences organically. Ship: 3 weeks (creator onboarding + leaderboard page + auto-attribution API). Cost: $3K–6K/month. Volume ceiling: 10K–50K shoppers/month from sponsorship deals alone (pure distribution play, like Robinhood bankrolling YouTube creators).

3. **Waitlist Position-Jumping + "Founding 100" Scarcity** — Robinhood/Gmail-style: new signups get public ranked position ("You're #4,273 on the Founding Waitlist"). Each referral = jump 100 spots. First 100 to reach rank #1 get "Founding 100" badge (public profile, leaderboard pin, early access to premium features). The anxiety + status combo drives 5–10 referrals/user organically. Ship: 1 week. Cost: $0. Volume ceiling: 5K–10K users in 60 days (viral coefficient K ≈ 3–5, doubling every 10–14 days).

The next seven mechanics (ranked below) are viable but trade-off on complexity, cost, or volume ceiling. Mechanics 4–10 score well on defensibility (EstateSales.NET cannot copy them in <90 days) and serve as sequencing backfill for months 2–3.

**Critical caveat:** All mechanics depend on real-data credibility. Patrick currently has 1 real organizer + seed/fake data. Waitlist position-jumping works without real data (signups drive signups). Loot Drop + TikTok Creator rely on real organizers populating the platform. If Patrick doesn't 1:1 onboard 3–5 real organizers in the next 4 weeks, Loot Drop and TikTok Sponsorship stall. Waitlist-jumping is the only mechanic that works in parallel with organizer recruitment — ship that first.

## What "Viral" Means Here (Locked Definition)

**Viral coefficient K ≥ 1.5** — One user action triggers ≥1.5 downstream signups/shares with **zero added founder labor per user acquired**. This is not networking-effect (better with more users) — it's compounding growth.

**Founder-hour scaling (REJECTED):** No mechanic where Patrick's hours scale linearly with users. Rejected:
- Cold calling organizers (Patrick's hours × users) — ❌
- In-person QR card handouts (Patrick's hours × venues) — ❌
- Concierge onboarding (Patrick's hours × 1:1 sessions) — ❌
- Daily TikTok uploads by Patrick (Patrick's hours × posts) — ❌
- Reddit AMAs (Patrick's hours × threads) — ❌
- Manual influencer outreach (Patrick's hours × emails) — ❌

**Compounding mechanics (ACCEPTED):** Mechanics where one user's existence or action automatically pulls in N more users, with zero Patrick intervention per new user:
- Share link clicks (network effect) — ✅
- Notification push opens (content distribution) — ✅
- Waitlist position anxiety (status/scarcity) — ✅
- Creator on-camera use (parasocial distribution) — ✅
- Referral rewards (economic incentive) — ✅

## Top 5 Mechanics – Detailed Specs

### 1. Loot Drop Notification Cascade

**What it is:** Every Friday at 8 AM local time, the platform auto-generates and sends a push notification to all shoppers in a given zip: "🔥 Wildest Finds This Week in [City] — [Item Name] sold for $[X]. See more." Notification includes 1 hero image (highest-value item sold that week). Tap → `/sales/[saleId]?ref=loot-drop-cascade`. Each new shopper on the platform triggers notifications for future Fridays.

**Why it's viral (K = 2.5–4.0):**
- Shopper receives notification → forwards screenshot to friend group (WhatsApp, iMessage, group chat) → friend clicks → new shopper joins.
- No founder involvement. Pure data-driven.
- Volume scales with sale frequency: more organizers = more sales = more viral notifications. Self-reinforcing loop.

**Why it's visceral:**
- The *specific item* (e.g., "Rolex found for $40") is the gut punch, not the platform.
- $X price tag + image creates screenshot-forwarding moment ("You won't BELIEVE what this person found").
- Urgency framing ("This Week") + emoji ("🔥") triggers FOMO.

**Build path:**
1. Create `generateWeeklyLootDropContent` service (40h, database query + rendering):
   - Query items published last 7 days, grouped by zip.
   - Rank by (actualPrice ÷ estimatedValue) descending — highest ROI flip.
   - Render hero item card with image + price + item name.
   - Return { saleId, itemId, heroImageUrl, priceTag, itemName, city } per zip.
2. Wire into existing Loot Drop push infrastructure (no new tables, no new endpoints):
   - `lootDropService.ts` already sends authenticated pushes to shoppers_by_zip.
   - Plug in weekly scheduler at `packages/backend/src/cron/fridayLootDrops.ts`.
   - Push title: `"🔥 Wildest Finds This Week in [city]"`.
   - Push body: `"[itemName] sold for $[X]. See more."`.
   - Deep link: `/sales/[saleId]?ref=loot-drop-cascade&itemId=[itemId]`.
3. Analytics:
   - Log notification send (existing lootDropMetrics table).
   - Track ?ref=loot-drop-cascade clicks in analytics.ts.
   - Chart weekly DAU lift per metro.

**Launch plan (7 days):**
- **Day 1:** Deploy `generateWeeklyLootDropContent` service.
- **Day 2:** Wire scheduler + push template.
- **Day 3–4:** Staging test (seed 3 test organizers with 10 test items each, manually trigger Friday push).
- **Day 5:** Deploy to production.
- **Day 6:** First automated Friday push fires.
- **Day 7:** Measure opens/clicks/new-shopper signups.

**Kill scenarios + mitigations:**
- **Real-data desert:** If <3 active organizers, notifications stop. *Mitigation:* Run in parallel with organizer recruitment. Seed 3 real organizers weeks 1–2 of this spike.
- **Low item velocity:** If organizers publish <5 items/week, hero selection fails. *Mitigation:* Fallback to highest-value item (estimatedValue) if no sales data yet.
- **Push fatigue/unsubscribe:** Shoppers disable push after 3 weeks. *Mitigation:* A/B test frequency (weekly vs. bi-weekly). Track unsubscribe rate weekly. Pause if >10% unsubscribe.

### 2. TikTok Creator Sponsorship + Auto-Attribution Loop

**What it is:** Patrick identifies 5–10 mid-tier estate-sale, antique, or vintage resale creators (50K–500K followers on TikTok). Sponsorships are straightforward: $500–2K per month per creator to use FindA.Sale in their existing content ("I listed this Victorian chair on FindA.Sale and it sold for $200 in 2 hours"). No scripts, no brand deals — just "use this app in your videos." FindA.Sale auto-tracks when a creator's TikTok/YouTube video links to the platform (via UTM params in creator's linktree or bio link). Weekly "Creator Hauls" leaderboard auto-updates with top creators by referral traffic + engagement.

**Why it's viral (K = 2.0–3.5):**
- Creator uploads video using app → creator's 100K–500K followers see it → 2–5% click through bio link → new shoppers join.
- Network effect: creators compete for top leaderboard rank, post more frequently, drive more traffic.
- No founder cold outreach. Payment + leaderboard drive creator engagement.

**Why it's visceral:**
- Creators talking about *finding* items on their platform (inherently entertaining content).
- FindA.Sale appears in context of aspirational resale (antiques, vintage) — premium positioning.
- Leaderboard rank + public attribution feeds parasocial relationship between creator and followers.

**Build path:**
1. Creator leaderboard page (8h, UI only):
   - `pages/shopper/creators/leaderboard.tsx` — table: Creator Name | TikTok Handle | Followers | Referrals Last 30D | Revenue This Month.
   - Route: `/creators?tab=top-referrers`.
   - Sort by referrals_count DESC. Top 3 get badges ("🥇 Creator", "🥈 Creator", "🥉 Creator").
   - Link each creator handle → verified TikTok/YouTube/Instagram profile (external link).
2. Creator verification + UTM tracking (12h, backend):
   - Create `CreatorPartner` table: { creatorId: String, tiktokHandle: String, youtubeChannel: String, instagramHandle: String, monthlySpend: Int, activeSince: DateTime, @@unique([tiktokHandle]) }.
   - Modify analytics.ts tracking: when `?utm_source=creator&utm_medium=[creatorHandle]` detected, increment `CreatorPartner.referralsCount`.
   - Create POST `/api/creators/register` (only Patrick can call, no auth check — manual onboarding):
     - Body: { tiktokHandle, monthlySpend, notes }.
     - Creates CreatorPartner record.
   - Create GET `/api/creators/leaderboard` (public):
     - Returns top 20 creators by referralsCount DESC.
     - Includes leaderboard position + monthly payout status.
3. Referral payout automation (0h, manual initially):
   - Patrick identifies 5–10 creators manually, DMing them or emailing custom offer.
   - Each creator gets a unique UTM code: `?utm_source=creator&utm_medium=[creatorHandle]`.
   - Post creation, creator pastes link in TikTok bio or linktree.
   - Weekly, Patrick exports referral data from CreatorPartner table, wires payment via Stripe.

**Launch plan (14 days):**
- **Week 1, Day 1–2:** Build creator leaderboard page + CreatorPartner table migration.
- **Week 1, Day 3–4:** Wire analytics.ts UTM tracking. Stage test with Patrick TikTok account.
- **Week 1, Day 5:** Deploy to production. Leaderboard live but empty.
- **Week 1, Day 6–7:** Patrick reaches out to 10 creators via email/DM. Offers: "$800/mo to post 2 FindA.Sale videos/week. Your referral link: [utm]."
- **Week 2, Day 1–7:** Creators onboard, start posting. Patrick monitors dashboard daily.
- **Week 2, Day 8–14:** First referral data populates leaderboard. Creators see public rank, post more content.

**Kill scenarios + mitigations:**
- **Creator burnout:** Creators post twice, then stop. *Mitigation:* Offer weekly bonuses for top referrer (+$100 if rank #1 that week). Make competition gamified.
- **TikTok algo shadow-ban:** Creator videos don't reach followers. *Mitigation:* Suggest creators optimize captions/hashtags. Share top-performing creator content to FindA.Sale social accounts (meta-distribution).
- **Low click-through rate (<1%):** Creators have audience but traffic doesn't convert. *Mitigation:* A/B test landing page for creator traffic (simpler sign-up, pre-filled "saw on [creator name]'s TikTok" context).
- **Platform policy violation:** TikTok flags videos as undisclosed sponsorship. *Mitigation:* Require creators to use #ad or #sponsored in captions. Use official TikTok Creator Fund dashboard if available.

### 3. Waitlist Position-Jumping + Founding 100 Badge

**What it is:** New signups land on a public waitlist page showing their position ("You're #4,273 on the Founding Waitlist"). Each friend invited via unique referral link = jump 100 positions (public leaderboard updates in real-time). First 100 users to reach #1 position earn "Founding 100" badge (profile badge + leaderboard pin + early access to premium features, e.g., free Hunt Pass month 1).

**Why it's viral (K = 3.0–5.0):**
- Signup creates anxiety ("I'm 4,273th place") + motivation ("Each friend = -100 spots").
- Viral loop: user invites 10 friends to climb 10 ranks → each friend invites 10 → exponential.
- No real data required (works with just signups).
- Robinhood waitlist famously drove 10M signups in 2 years via this mechanic.

**Why it's visceral:**
- Ranking + scarcity ("Only 100 Founding badges") create FOMO.
- Public leaderboard position is visible to all users (social proof + status).
- Status badge creates perceived "early access" prestige.

**Build path:**
1. Waitlist position tracking (4h):
   - Add `User` fields: `waitlistPosition: Int`, `waitlistScore: Int (= position after referrals)`.
   - Create `Referral` table: { referrerId: String, refereeId: String, createdAt: DateTime, @@unique([referrerId, refereeId]) }.
   - On signup, set `waitlistPosition` = current max + 1. `waitlistScore` = waitlistPosition.
2. Referral link generation + score recalc (2h):
   - Create `/api/referral/generate` → returns unique referral code: `finda.sale/join?ref=[randomString]`.
   - Store `Referral` record: { referrerId, refereeId, createdAt }.
   - Recalc script runs every hour: for each user, count referrals where createdAt > 24h ago, decrement waitlistScore by (count × 100), but never below 1.
   - If waitlistScore <= 100, set `isFounding100: true`. Emit notification: "🎖️ You made the Founding 100!"
3. Waitlist position page (4h):
   - `pages/waitlist.tsx`:
     - Auth required (logged-in shoppers only).
     - Hero: "You're #[waitlistScore] on the Founding Waitlist" (large, bold).
     - Subtext: "Refer [10 - refCount] more friends to reach the Founding 100."
     - Share button: copy referral link, share to iMessage/WhatsApp/Email/Twitter.
     - Leaderboard below: "Friends on Waitlist" — shows referees this user invited, sorted by signup order.
     - If `isFounding100: true`, show badge: "🎖️ You're Founding 100! Early access unlocked."
4. Founding 100 perk activation (0h, no code changes):
   - Patrick manually activates perks on backend by setting `foundingHuntPassExpiry: Date.now() + 30*24*60*60*1000` (1 free month).
   - Frontend checks this field on `/shopper/loyalty` page; if active + not yet claimed, show toast: "Your Founding 100 benefit: Free Hunt Pass for 1 month. Claim now?"

**Launch plan (1 day):**
- **Hour 1–2:** Ship waitlist position + referral tracking.
- **Hour 3–4:** Deploy waitlist page + share button.
- **Hour 5:** Announce on Twitter/email: "New Founding Waitlist launched. Join and refer friends to jump the queue."
- **Hour 6–24:** Organic signups + shares as users fight for Founding 100 rank.

**Kill scenarios + mitigations:**
- **Saturation:** Reaches 100 Founding badges too fast (<1 week). *Mitigation:* Top-up strategy — if Founding 100 fills in <3 weeks, create "Founding 250" tier (next 150 positions). Keep scarcity moving.
- **Gaming:** Users create multiple accounts to inflate referrals. *Mitigation:* Add fraud check: if >3 referrals from same IP within 24h, flag as spam. Require phone verification to create account (or email verification only, low friction).
- **Low engagement:** Users sign up, never invite anyone. *Mitigation:* Email campaign: "You're #[pos]. Invite [N] friends to jump to Founding 100 in [days]."

### 4. Auto-Generated Weekly "Wildest Finds" Reels (Social Export)

**What it is:** Every Sunday, the platform auto-generates a 30–60 second short-form video ("Wildest Finds Reel") showing the top 5–10 items sold that week (images, prices, GIFs, captions). Video is posted automatically to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts via FindA.Sale social accounts. Each video is optimized for that platform (aspect ratio, captions, trending audio). Users see "Found by [Creator Name] on FindA.Sale" watermark. Users can download the reel for their own channels or share the link.

**Why it's viral (K = 1.5–2.5):**
- Video reaches FindA.Sale TikTok followers (assumed 5K–10K by month 3).
- Each reel drives 2–5% click-through to platform.
- Viewers share reel to group chats → friends ask "What app is this?" → new signups.

**Why it's visceral:**
- Reel format is inherently shareable (TikTok's strongest distribution).
- Price-flips ($10 necklace → $300 sale) are inherently "wow" moments.
- Audio (trending TikTok sounds) + editing (fast cuts, emojis) creates premium production feel.

**Build path:**
1. Reel generation service (24h, Python + Anthropic):
   - Query top 10 items sold last 7 days by (actualPrice ÷ estimatedValue).
   - For each item: extract image, price, category, creator name.
   - Prompt Anthropic video-generation API (if available) OR export sequence to video template library:
     - Fallback: use Anthropic to generate captions + trending audio suggestions.
     - Use `ffmpeg` or Cloudinary video API to composite item images + captions + transitions.
   - Output: `.mp4` file, 1080×1920px (vertical), 30–60 seconds, with trending audio.
2. Social posting automation (8h):
   - Create `SocialSchedule` table: { videoId, platform (TIKTOK|INSTAGRAM|YOUTUBE), scheduledAt, status, externalId }.
   - Use TikTok Business API + Instagram Graph API + YouTube Data API to post reel.
   - Handle auth via OAuth tokens stored in env (TikTok requires business account).
   - Cron: every Sunday 8 PM, generate reel + post to all platforms.
3. Frontend download/share UI (0h, already in platform):
   - Reel appears on `/trending` page or `/inspiration` page with download button (CTA: "Download & Share").
   - Share buttons: copy link, Twitter, TikTok (external).

**Launch plan (3 days):**
- **Day 1:** Build video generation service. Locally test with 5 seed items.
- **Day 2:** Wire social posting APIs. Stage test posting to dev TikTok account.
- **Day 3:** Deploy to production. First Sunday reel auto-generates + posts.

**Kill scenarios + mitigations:**
- **Low video quality:** Auto-generated reels look amateurish. *Mitigation:* Use professional template library (Descript, Runway ML, or hire video editor to create 3 templates). Rotate templates weekly for variety.
- **Audio licensing:** Trending audio on TikTok is platform-specific (can't repost to YouTube). *Mitigation:* Use royalty-free audio library (Epidemic Sound, AudioJungle) instead. Lose some virality but avoid takedowns.
- **Real-data dependency:** No high-value sales = boring reel. *Mitigation:* Fallback to "most-saved items" or "most-commented items" as tiebreaker.

### 5. Shopper Bounty Board (Hunt + Match)

**What it is:** Shoppers create a public "wishlist" or "bounty" for items they're hunting: "Looking for vintage leather midcentury chair, brown/tan, <$200." The bounty is a public card on `/bounties`. When an organizer lists a matching item, FindA.Sale matches via tags/category/price and notifies both bounty-creator and organizer: "Someone is looking for exactly what you listed!" Bounty-creator gets notified → clicks bounty card → lands on matching item → buys. Organizer gets notified → sees match → relists faster or raises inventory.

**Why it's viral (K = 1.8–2.2):**
- Bounty-creator posts ("Looking for X") → friends see bounty → reply with sightings ("I saw that at [estate sale]") → friends click bounty link → new shoppers join.
- Organizers see bounty matches → list more items → more sales → more shoppers get notified.
- Two-sided network effect (shoppers pull organizers, organizers pull shoppers).

**Why it's visceral:**
- The "match" moment ("I found what you're looking for!") is deeply satisfying.
- Organizers want to fulfill bounties (ego + sales).
- Shoppers feel heard (their specific want gets attention).

**Build path:**
1. Bounty model + API (32h):
   - Create `Bounty` table: { id, creatorId, title, description, category, minPrice, maxPrice, tags[], status (ACTIVE|FULFILLED), createdAt, updatedAt }.
   - POST `/api/bounties` — create bounty (auth required).
   - GET `/api/bounties?status=ACTIVE` — list all active bounties.
   - PUT `/api/bounties/[id]/fulfill` — mark as fulfilled (only bounty creator or organizer can call).
2. Match algorithm (16h):
   - Create `matchBounty` service:
     - On item publish (existing `publishItem` webhook), compare item against all active bounties.
     - Match criteria: (category match OR tag overlap) AND (price in range) AND (condition matches user preference, if stated).
     - If match score > 70%, create notification: "Found a match for your bounty '[title]'!"
     - Notify both bounty-creator and organizer.
   - Store matches in `BountyMatch` table: { bountyId, itemId, matchScore, createdAt }.
3. Bounty detail page (8h):
   - `pages/bounties/[id].tsx` — show bounty details + list matching items below.
   - "Create Similar Bounty" button for shoppers who land here.
   - Bounty creator + organizer of matched items appear in sidebar (for follow/contact).
4. Frontend bounty creation modal (0h, reuse existing modal patterns):
   - Input: title, description, category picker, price range slider, condition preference.
   - Post modal to `/api/bounties`.

**Launch plan (21 days):**
- **Week 1:** Build bounty model + API + match algorithm. Stage test with 10 seed bounties + 5 test items.
- **Week 2:** Ship bounty detail page + creation modal. Deploy to production.
- **Week 2–3:** Promote bounty board on `/trending` page. Seed 5 real bounties from Patrick's friends/beta organizers (create bounties for items that will soon be listed).
- **Week 3:** First organic bounty matches fire. Measure bounty-creation rate + match success rate.

**Kill scenarios + mitigations:**
- **Low bounty creation:** Shoppers don't understand the feature. *Mitigation:* Onboarding tooltip on `/trending` page. Email blast: "Create a bounty for items you're hunting — organizers want to help!"
- **Poor match accuracy:** Matches are false positives. *Mitigation:* Add manual review step for organizer before notification fires. Or require min match score > 85% instead of 70%.
- **Bounty spam/abuse:** Users create nonsense bounties. *Mitigation:* Bounties require 50+ XP or verified email. Limit to 5 active bounties per user.

## Bottom 7+ Mechanics – One-Paragraph Summaries

**6. PR Wire Blast (Cision/PRNewswire) — K = 1.2–1.8, 4h eng**  
Single day of work: Patrick writes press release ("FindA.Sale Launches Secondary Sale App for Estate & Yard Sale Organizers"), submits via Cision or PRNewswire ($500–1K one-time cost), auto-distributes to 200+ local news affiliates (AP, NPR, Patch, local business journals). Generates 50–200 inbound click-throughs from journalists/readers. Low viral coefficient but zero founder hours after submission. **Deprioritize:** One-time spike only; no compounding. Use as Month 2 publicity stunt after organic mechanics are live.

**7. Organizer-to-Organizer Referral Bounty — K = 1.5–2.0, 12h eng, $20K/mo cost**  
Patrick pays each organizer a bounty for referring a new organizer who publishes 5+ sales. Bounty: $500 first organizer, $250 second, $100 each thereafter. Creates FOMO among organizers ("If I refer 5 friends, I make $1.75K"). Organizers become micro-affiliates. **Deprioritize:** Requires budget + scales Patrick's payment engineering. Revisit Month 3 if organic mechanics slow.

**8. Screenshot-Share Viral Loop ("I Found This Rolex") — K = 2.0–3.0, 24h eng**  
Add "Save & Share" button on item detail (existing share infrastructure). Shopper takes screenshot of Rolex item selling for $40 (actual example), text reads "I Found This Rolex for $40 on FindA.Sale. It was listed as an estate sale find." Saved to shopper's camera roll. Shopper shares to TikTok/Instagram Story. Friends see it, click link in bio → download app. Essentially TikTok Creator Sponsorship but user-generated. **Deprioritize:** Lower volume than paid sponsorships (depends on user adoption). Implement Week 2 as complement to Mechanic 2.

**9. eBay Sync Auto-Content per Metro (Pre-Organizer) — K = 1.0–1.5, 16h eng**  
Even before real organizers exist in a city, populate that city's landing page with "Top eBay Sales in [City] This Week" aggregated from eBay API (sold comps for items in that city). Creates perception of lived-in marketplace. Links to marketplace pages (not organizer sales yet). **Deprioritize:** Low viral coefficient. Content is eBay's, not FindA.Sale's — low brand-building. Use as filler content on empty city pages only.

**10. Seasonal "Last-Call" Rush Alerts (Time Scarcity) — K = 1.5–2.0, 12h eng**  
Every evening at 6 PM, platform sends push: "⏰ [N] items ending in 2 hours — Last chance to bid!" Notification includes 3-item carousel (highest-value items closing today). Creates FOMO + urgency. Shoppers frantically bid → new winners → notifications ripple. **Deprioritize:** Medium complexity, medium payoff. Implement Week 2 alongside Mechanic 1 (both notification-based).

**11. Creator User-Generated Content Swaps — K = 1.3–1.8, 28h eng, $500/mo cost**  
Instead of paying creators, offer content swap: FindA.Sale features creator's videos on platform social accounts (meta-distribution) + creator features FindA.Sale in their content. Creator gets audience, platform gets content. Lower cost than Mechanic 2. **Deprioritize:** Requires sophisticated content curation + creator coordination. Implement Month 2 as low-cost alternative if creator sponsorship underperforms.

**12. Gamified "Tag Your Friend" Treasure Hunt — K = 1.2–1.6, 16h eng**
