## Structural Decisions

### Why These 19 Targets?

- **NESA, NAA, NASMM, ISA:** Trade association outreach (S641 §5.2 explicit recommendation). Highest institutional credibility, slowest response, highest partnership value.
- **Nick Loper, Codie Sanchez:** Large-audience content creators covering entrepreneurship / small business (referenced in advisory-outreach-drafts.md as Wave 5 targets). Email templates in that file already existed but needed repurposing for partnership angle (vs. advisory feedback).
- **Podcast/Newsletter hosts:** 8 targets with direct reach to organizers, downsize specialists, or adjacent audiences (Estate Professionals, Empowered Elder, Downsizing Roadmap, Consignment Chats, etc.). Blend of S641 recommendations + advisory-outreach-drafts.md coverage (some already drafted, repositioned here as partnerships).
- **Educational platforms & templates:** Pat Walls (case study opportunity), SRES Realtor (referral template), Senior Settlers (resource partnership). Lower urgency, longer sales cycle, education/integration-focused.

### Email Structure (All Drafts Follow)

1. **Opener:** Reference what THEY do, not what we need
2. **Bridge:** Connect their world to the estate sale organizer problem
3. **Ask:** One specific CTA (partnership explore, call, guest episode, case study, resource collab)
4. **Proof/Context:** Link to https://finda.sale/video (or reference podcast guest angle)
5. **Close:** No pressure, available for call/discussion
6. **Sign:** Patrick Deseranno or "The FindA.Sale Team" + FindA.Sale

None of the drafts:
- List features ("inventory, pricing, photo workflows")
- Use "would you be open to" phrasing
- Mention "AI," "smart," or "auto" in context of the product (kept those words out entirely in partnership asks — different audience than creator advisory)
- Offer free trial or account signups
- Request equity, affiliate, or shoutouts in the opening ask

### When Responses Come In

**If "yes" to call/meeting:**
- Reply within 24 hours with 3 time slot options (next 10 days)
- Example: "Wed 2pm ET, Thu 10am ET, or Fri 3pm ET — let me know what works"
- Keep reply to 1–2 sentences max (they're already interested)

**If "what specifically do you offer?":**
- Keep answer to 2–3 sentences; let them dig deeper in call
- Example: "Operational software that automates inventory, pricing, photos, and checkout — saves operators 10–15 hours per sale. Happy to show you in a call what this looks like in practice."
- Do NOT list features in email reply

**If no response after 10 days:**
- One follow-up email with different angle (new pain point angle, new question, reference to something they said in past)
- If still no response after 14 days total, mark as "cold" and move on
- Save every no/no-response for pattern analysis
