## 1. Facebook Group Post Templates

### Template A: Workflow Tip (No Product Mention)

**Topic:** Time-saving techniques

---

"Question for the group: how long does it take you to write descriptions for a 50-item sale?

I used to spend 2–3 hours on this. Switched my process to batch-writing (group all items by category first, write descriptions together, then upload). Got it down to 45 minutes. 

Biggest time-saver for me: taking all the photos first, THEN writing, instead of doing both for each item. Makes you write faster because your brain stays in one category.

What's your system? Curious if anyone's found something faster."

**Why this works:** Asks for input, shares a specific tactic, invites others to share. Positions you as thoughtful and peer-focused, not sales-y.

---

### Template B: Answering a Common Pain Point

**Scenario:** Someone posts "How do you price estate sale items? I always underestimate value."

---

"Good question. I started using eBay's sold listings (not asking price — sold prices). For any item, I search for 3 similar pieces, check what they actually sold for, average it, then drop my starting price 10–15% to encourage bidding.

Works better than guessing, and it's way faster than pricing one at a time. Probably saves me 30 min per sale.

For high-value items (furniture, art, jewelry), I'll check a couple of other sources too, but eBay is usually accurate.

What approach do you use?"

**Why this works:** Specific, actionable, demonstrates real experience. You're teaching, not pitching. Ends with a question that keeps conversation going.

---

### Template C: Discussion Starter (Mining for Pain Points)

**Topic:** Efficiency benchmarks

---

"Running a quick informal survey. How many hours do you spend on the entire listing process for a typical 50–100 item sale? 

I'm talking photos, descriptions, pricing, uploading, cross-posting to eBay/Facebook/etc.

I'm asking because workflow efficiency is huge for profitability, and I'd love to benchmark what's normal. Planning to synthesize responses and share back with the group."

**Why this works:** Positions you as data-driven and thoughtful. When people respond, you learn their pain points. You now know which organizers struggle with which part of the workflow — perfect intel for future warm intros.

---

### Template D: Sharing a Useful Resource (Soft Authority Build)

**Topic:** Templates, systems, or tools

---

"For anyone manually managing inventory in spreadsheets—I finally automated my CSV upload to eBay. Saves me 20 minutes per sale on formatting.

I'm happy to share the template if anyone wants it. DM me or reply here and I'll send it over. (It's just a structured CSV format that eBay accepts directly, nothing fancy.)

Also curious: does anyone use a different system that works better? Always looking to optimize."

**Why this works:** Gives without asking for anything. People appreciate this. They DM you or reply. You're building a direct contact list of engaged organizers. They know your name now.
