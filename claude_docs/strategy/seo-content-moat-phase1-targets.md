## Overview

Phase 1 ships 500 pages across 5 content categories. This document lists the exact hitlist for each category with target keywords, search volume estimates, and implementation priority.

**Total breakdown:**
- **Pricing Guides (50 pages):** Real eBay sold-comp data, condition tiers, brand premiums
- **Identification Guides (50 pages):** How to identify marks, fakes, maker details
- **Per-City × Per-Category (250 pages):** Top 25 metros × 10 categories, fully auto-generated
- **Buying Guides & How-Tos (50 pages):** Evergreen content for shoppers + organizers
- **Trend Reports (100 pages):** Monthly refresh templates + seasonal variants
- **TOTAL: 500 pages**
