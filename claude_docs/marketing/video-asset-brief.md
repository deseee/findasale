# Before/After Demo Video — Asset Brief

**Purpose:** Primary demand generation piece. Show the time savings on inventory setup (3-4 hours → 27 minutes).

**Status:** Ready to film immediately. No post-production required (natural sound, simple captions, minimal editing).

---

## Video Specs

- **Duration:** 2:30–3:00 (people will share 27-second clips after)
- **Orientation:** Landscape (16:9) for desktop/YouTube, crop to 9:16 for Instagram Reels/TikTok
- **Audio:** Natural sound (phone taps, camera clicks) + Patrick's voice explaining as he goes. NO music. NO voiceover.
- **Captions:** White sans-serif text on black background (readable at any size). Appears on every interaction.
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum (phone footage is fine if well-lit)
- **Format:** .mp4 (save to ~/Downloads or Google Drive for instant sharing)

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Setup (0:00–0:10)
**What you're showing:** Starting from zero. No inventory yet.

**Visual:**
- Clock on-screen showing start time (e.g., 2:47pm)
- Patrick or collaborator with phone
- Real items (furniture, dishes, collectibles — not staged)
- One-line on-screen caption: "Starting fresh. No inventory yet."

**Audio:**
Patrick: "This is a 150-item estate sale I'm helping coordinate. Normally, photography and setup takes 4 hours. Let me show you what's changed."

---

### Scene 2: Photo Capture (0:10–0:35)
**What you're showing:** Fast photo capture with auto-tagging appearing in real-time.

**Visual:**
- Point phone at item
- Tap to capture photo
- Immediately show the auto-tag result appearing on screen
- Repeat 5-6 times with different items (chair, dishes, lamp, shelf, etc.)
- Caption on-screen: "Point, tap, done. Auto-tags appear instantly."

**Audio:**
Patrick: "Photograph the item — takes 5 seconds. The system looks at the photo and suggests the category. Bedroom Furniture. Kitchenware. Collectibles. Usually it's right."

---

### Scene 3: Review & Confirm (0:35–1:10)
**What you're showing:** Organizer reviewing auto-suggestions. Skip, adjust, or confirm.

**Visual:**
- Show the review screen with auto-suggested tag and price
- Organizer confirms (green checkmark)
- Next item: organizer adjusts the price (shows quick edit)
- Next item: organizer skips the auto-price and enters custom price
- Caption on-screen: "Skip, adjust, or confirm. Your call."

**Audio:**
Patrick: "Each item shows the auto-suggested category and price. You have three options: confirm it, adjust it, or skip the auto-suggestion and enter your own price. Takes 2-3 seconds per item."

---

### Scene 4: Bulk Actions (1:10–1:35)
**What you're showing:** Publishing all items at once. Sale goes live.

**Visual:**
- Show the inventory list (50+ items visible, scrolling)
- Scroll to bottom
- Hit "Publish Sale" button (or "Go Live")
- Confirmation screen appears
- Caption on-screen: "Bulk publish. Sale goes live in seconds."

**Audio:**
Patrick: "Once you've reviewed the items, you publish the whole sale at once. No item-by-item publishing. Click, confirm, done."

---

### Scene 5: Live Proof (1:35–2:15)
**What you're showing:** The actual sale is now live and searchable on FindA.Sale.

**Visual:**
- Switch to app view showing the sale
- Show the sale landing page (location, date, photo grid)
- Scroll through item listings
- Show one or two items with photos and details
- Show the sale appearing in a search or map view
- Caption on-screen: "All 150 items are now discoverable and searchable."

**Audio:**
Patrick: "The sale is live. All 150 items are indexed, searchable by category, and visible to buyers in the area. Serious buyers can see everything now."

---

### Scene 6: The Outcome (2:15–2:50)
**What you're showing:** Time elapsed. Total time from start to live.

**Visual:**
- Show the clock from Scene 1 (2:47pm start)
- Show current time (3:14pm end)
- Calculate on-screen: "27 minutes."
- Overlay text: "What normally takes 3–4 hours: 27 minutes."
- Show a real organizer nodding or smiling (if filming with someone else)
- Final caption: "No, you're not watching at 4x speed."

**Audio:**
Patrick: "From zero to live in 27 minutes. Not 4 hours. Not condensed video. Real time. That's what this workflow enables."

## Filming Notes

### Location
- Real sale environment (garage, estate, warehouse, flea market setup)
- Natural lighting (don't film in dark corners)
- Items visible and interesting

### Phone
- Use a decent phone camera (iPhone 12+ or Android equivalent)
- Hold steady (use a small tripod or prop phone on a stand if needed)
- Landscape orientation throughout

### Captions
- Simple white text (no fancy fonts or animations)
- Appear on-screen for 3-4 seconds each
- Readable even at thumbnail size (YouTube)

### Editing
- Minimal. Just stitching scenes together.
- Keep natural audio (no background music)
- If you want, add a simple fade between scenes (optional)

### Audio
- Patrick explains as he goes (natural, conversational tone)
- Not a voiceover recorded later — record audio live while filming
- If noise is bad, re-record voiceover in post (but keep it sounding natural)
