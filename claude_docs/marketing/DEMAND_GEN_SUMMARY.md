# Demand Generation Playbook — Executive Summary

**Goal:** Organizers encounter FindA.Sale through content, community, and peer recommendation. By the time they talk to anyone, they're already sold.

**Channels:** Peer-to-peer text/email, Facebook groups, LinkedIn, video (YouTube), organic word-of-mouth referral.

**Budget:** ~$0 (just time and content creation). No ads, no sales team yet.

**Timeline:** Start filming video this week. Community posting begins next week. Measurable growth in 3-6 months.

## Success Metrics

**Track monthly:**
- New organizer signups from each channel (referral link, landing page, video, groups)
- Video views and shares (which clips perform best?)
- Community post engagement (comments, shares, group activity)
- Landing page conversion rate (visitors → free trial)
- Referral program take rate (% of new signups using a referral link)

**Target (3-6 months):**
- 20+ organizers acquired through organic channels
- Landing page converts 15-20% of visitors
- 30%+ of new signups come from referral program
- Community posts get 15+ genuine comments each
- Organizers proactively sharing with peers (word-of-mouth loop starts)

---

## What NOT to Do

- ❌ Cold email outreach ("Hi, I built a tool for event organizers")
- ❌ Paid ads yet (wait until organic channel is proven)
- ❌ Sales team (too early, would break the peer tone)
- ❌ One big push (this is sustained, 1-2 posts/week for 3 months)
- ❌ Generic SaaS marketing ("Our innovative platform..."). Keep it peer-focused.
- ❌ Oversell (if inventory setup isn't YOUR pain, don't claim it's the biggest pain)

## The Core Insight

Organizers don't want to be sold to. They want to know: "Does this solve my actual problem?" 

The most powerful sales channel is a peer saying, "I've been doing this for 10 years, I tried this tool, it saved me 3 hours. Worth a shot."

Everything in this playbook is built to make THAT conversation happen naturally.

## Questions?

- **"Will this actually work?"** — Yes, if you execute consistently. It's peer-driven, not paid. It takes 3-6 months. But it works because it's authentic.

- **"What if I don't have time to post 2x/week?"** — Start with 1x/week. Consistency matters more than frequency. One genuine post beats five generic ones.

- **"Can I hire someone to do the community posts?"** — Not yet. The credibility comes from you posting as the founder. Once you have testimonials and proof, consider delegating. But not now.

- **"What if the video doesn't get traction?"** — Post it everywhere anyway. Even if it gets 100 views total, 10 people might try it, and 1-2 might become customers. The video also becomes reference material for your conversations.

- **"How do I know which sale type to focus on?"** — Start with all of them. The playbook includes templates for estate, garage, auction, and flea. See which one resonates most in your early customers, then lean into that.
