**Spot check — WARM tier:** ~75% legitimate resale. Included: GC5 Estate Services, C2 Estate Sales, Morphy Auctions, Victoria's Vintage Estate Sales, SalesbyHelen, Ben Hersh Estate Sales, Travis Mitchell Auctions, St. Louis Estate Sales. Noise: Ladha Shafik Real Estate Agent (BC), Pouches & More Online Shopping Mall (BC) — both Canada, already excluded by outreach cron. The Cash Store (pawn chain). Flea Market Boys (Melrose Trading Post) — borderline but resale-adjacent.

**Spot check — HOT tier:** Mostly good. Includes Pacific Auction House, Legacy Auctions & Estate Sales, Clearing House Estate Sales, Bird's Nest Auctions, Oliver's Auction LLC. False positives: Goodwill Donation Center (Houston), The RealReal (luxury, not estate sale scale). Score=50 for all (floor of HOT tier threshold).

**NY Phase 2:** Zero records with `licenseState='NY'` returned — likely already filtered or not persisted with that field. NY noise in COLD appears to be from a different pathway.

**Verdict:** WARM tier is sendable — mostly legitimate resale businesses, Canada already excluded by cron. COLD tier has significant non-resale noise; don't send broadly. Actionable first cohort: WARM with `contactEmail IS NOT NULL` (~750 estimated). High-confidence cohort: 197 orgs with `emailDiscoveryConfidence > 0.60` across any tier.
