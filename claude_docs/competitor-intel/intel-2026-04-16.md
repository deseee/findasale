## Market Signals

NAR reported existing-home sales down 3.6% in March — a slow housing market historically correlates with higher estate sale volume as families liquidate assets during life transitions without selling the house.

Baby Boomer downsizing wave is accelerating: households with $100k+ income are 2.5× more likely to hire a professional liquidator. Urban organizers 35% more likely to prefer online-only auctions (parking constraints). Both trends favor a mobile-first PWA approach.

Y2K/90s fashion, sports memorabilia, and quality small appliances (KitchenAid, Vitamix, All-Clad) continue to drive buyer demand at in-person sales. Large furniture and china still difficult to move.
