Small quality appliances (KitchenAid, Vitamix, All-Clad) and vintage/aesthetic items move fastest. Large formal furniture declining. Gen Z and Millennial buyers are the growth edge — they want discovery, not directories.

## Top 3 Actionable Insights

1. **EstateSail now has AI photo enhancement AND Tap to Pay.** The organizer-side feature gap is narrowing. Marketing must shift emphasis to what EstateSail will never have: a shopper discovery layer, multi-channel listing, and a price point below $190/month. Lead with the demand side — "bring buyers to your sale" — not feature checklists.

2. **Stripe cross-border payouts are live.** FindA.Sale's Connect integration could now support organizers in Canada and Europe with minimal additional work. Low-effort future expansion unlock — flag to Architect for roadmap consideration.

3. **9.1% of households downsizing is a structural pipeline, not a cycle.** Outreach copy should lean into inevitability framing: "The wave of estate sales is here — the organizers who build their digital presence now capture the buyers forming habits today." Urgency without hype.
