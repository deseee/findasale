## Competitor Updates

**EstateSales.NET** — No new organizer features. Last app update March 10 (dark mode only). Platform remains buyer-focused. Organizer requests (better catalog tools, sorting, analytics) continue to go unaddressed publicly.

**PROSALE** — Showing up on GetApp, SoftwareAdvice, SourceForge, and GoodFirms with 2026 review profiles. Full-featured: POS, photographic inventory, online store with real-time sync, analytics by category/zip code, bulk email marketing. Strong comparison-site presence.

**Gavelbase** — AI cataloging platform for estate sale resellers gaining visibility in search results. Uses vision AI for lot identification and description generation. New entrant to watch for comparison-site displacement.

**Aravenda / AuctionMethod / Rosy** — All appearing in 2026 "best estate sale software" roundups. Category is crowding at the comparison-site layer even if individual competitors lack FindA.Sale's mobile-first UX.

## Top 3 Actionable Insights

1. **MaxSold's photo upload bugs are a 2026 complaint, not a 2025 one.** "Partial image display when uploading from phone gallery" is showing up in January 2026 reviews. FindA.Sale's reliable photo-first workflow is a direct, timely messaging opportunity — not just a feature comparison.

The differentiator is *reliability* — AI that doesn't require manual correction after every upload. FindA.Sale should shift messaging from "we have AI" to "AI that actually works the first time."

Organizers searching "estate sale software 2026" at the moment of decision will not find FindA.Sale. A single GetApp or SoftwareAdvice listing would fix this.
