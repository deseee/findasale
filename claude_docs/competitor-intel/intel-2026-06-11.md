# Weekly Competitor Intel — 2026-06-11

---

## Competitor Updates

**EstateSales.NET** — v3.54 still current (March 2026 bug fix only). No product investment signals. Nine weeks since any code push. Organizers continue requesting a "not interested" flag with color coding — only "favorite" exists. Stagnant.

**MaxSold** — No new features or announcements. Commission model still 20–30% of hammer price. Messaging unchanged. No market expansion signals beyond current 30-metro US/Canada footprint.

**EstateSales.org** — Silent. No updates.

**EstateSail (learn.estatesail.us)** — ⚠️ WATCH. Direct AI competitor now in market. v1.1.3 out with optimized iPad layouts. Features: AI photo→title/description/pricing/tags (same camera→AI pipeline as FindA.Sale), barcode label printing, QR check-in/pre-registration, real-time revenue tracking, team role-based access, web dashboard. Price: **$189.99/month**. This is the most direct feature overlap seen to date — organizer-side AI tagging is no longer a unique differentiator. FindA.Sale differentiation must lean into shopper discovery, multi-channel listing, and lower price point.

**Rosy (gorosy.co)** — CRM-forward: email marketing to shoppers, dynamic pricing from eBay/Facebook trends, contract management. No 2026 updates found. Complementary niche, not a head-to-head threat.

## Market Signals

Baby Boomer liquidation wave continues. IBISWorld confirms industry at $247.6M, up 7.5% YoY. 11,200 Americans turning 65 daily throughout 2026. Millennials and Gen Z flooding in as vintage buyers with disposable income — sustainability + uniqueness positioning resonates.

Facebook ad spend by estate sale/liquidation companies hit $18.4M in Q1 2026 alone (Meta Small Business Advertising Report). Average CPC: $0.67, conversion to in-person attendance: 4.2%. Organizers are spending big on digital — they need the platform to justify it.

AuctionNinja continues to implode: site crashes during live auctions, weeks of unanswered support, shill bidding concerns. Organizers actively shopping alternatives.

## Top 3 Actionable Insights

1. **EstateSail is now a direct AI feature competitor.** Camera→AI tagging at $189.99/month. FindA.Sale must stress what EstateSail lacks: shopper discovery, multi-channel cross-listing, and a pricing model that isn't $190/month. Lead marketing should highlight the discovery side and lower price point before EstateSail builds brand awareness.

2. **AuctionNinja's collapse is an acquisition window.** Organizers already looking for alternatives. A targeted outreach campaign naming "better support" and "no site crashes during sales" could convert frustrated AuctionNinja users directly. High intent, low friction.

3. **$18.4M in Facebook ad spend = organizers need ROI from digital.** Outreach messaging should connect FindA.Sale to their existing ad investment — "bring your Facebook audience to your FindA.Sale listing" — not pitch a new tool from scratch.
