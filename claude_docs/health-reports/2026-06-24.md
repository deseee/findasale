## Medium

- **Env var name mismatch: `GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY` vs `GG_API_KEY`** — `packages/backend/src/lib/placesService.ts:36` and `scripts/enrichContactEmails.ts:63` read `process.env.GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY`, but the local `.env` has `GG_API_KEY` instead. Both files have a graceful `|| ""` fallback, so Places lookups silently fall back to DuckDuckGo locally. Verify Railway env var name — if Railway also has `GG_API_KEY`, Places enrichment is disabled in production too. Fix: rename `.env.example` entry to `GG_API_KEY` (or vice versa) and confirm Railway matches.
