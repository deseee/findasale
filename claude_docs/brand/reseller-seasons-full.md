# Reseller Series — Two Full Seasons + Patrick's Official How-To Outline

Three things in this doc:
- **Season A — "Flipping for a Living"** (12 weeks, the money story)
- **Season B — "Sourced"** (12 weeks, the treasure-hunt lifestyle channel)
- **Patrick's track — "FindA.Sale, Actually Used"** (official-socials how-to outline, grounded in the existing FAQ/help docs)

Both host seasons run ~12 weeks (one filming day per week → 1 long-form + 6–8 verticals). Run them concurrently if you have two hosts, or back-to-back as Season 1 and Season 2 with one host. The product appears in use, never pitched. The word "AI" never appears on screen — the product's own language is **Auto Tags**, **Smart Pricing**, **Rapid Capture**. Sender/voice is the host's own; FindA.Sale is just the tool on her phone.

# SEASON A — "Flipping for a Living"

**Premise:** Can she turn thrift-and-flip into a real income? Every episode shows a running profit scoreboard on screen. The scoreboard is the retention engine — viewers come back to see the number move.

**Host voice:** Honest, scrappy, a little self-deprecating. Shows the losses as proudly as the wins.

**Season-long spine:** Start with $100. Goal posted in Ep.1: hit a $1,000-profit month by Week 12. The number on screen each episode is cumulative.

**Recurring on-screen elements:** the Scoreboard (spent / earned / net), "Best Find of the Week," "Biggest L of the Week," and a closing "Next week's goal" card.

### The 12 episodes

**Ep 1 — "$100 and a Dream"**
- Hook (0–3s, verbatim): *"This is $100. I'm going to try to turn it into a thousand. Week one starts now."*
- Arc: She explains the challenge, opens FindA.Sale to find the weekend's sales near her, maps a route, and does her first small buy. Ends with the first listings going up.
- Product touchpoint: discovery — browsing the map/search, following an organizer.
- Slices: the challenge announcement (TikTok), "what $100 actually buys you" haul (Reels), a 30s "how I find sales near me" (Shorts), the route-planning map reveal, first-listing time-lapse.

**Ep 2 — "The Estate Sale Goldmine"**
- Hook: *"I found this sale at 9pm last night. By noon I'd doubled my money."*
- Arc: A full sourcing trip to a promising estate sale she spotted the night before. The hunt, the haggling, the haul reveal, first sales landing.
- Product touchpoint: finding the sale the night before, scanning a QR sticker to check an item's listed price/condition on the spot.
- Slices: the late-night "I found a sale" hook, the in-aisle QR scan, "guess what I paid," haul reveal, one object's before/after.

**Ep 3 — "Everything I Bought Is Wrong"**
- Hook: *"I spent $80 this week. I've made back $11. Let's talk about it."*
- Arc: The flop episode. What didn't sell, why, what she misjudged. Maximum honesty = maximum trust. Ends with her re-pricing and re-listing.
- Product touchpoint: editing listings/prices after going live; checking Smart Pricing suggestions against what she'd set.
- Slices: "my biggest reselling mistakes," the re-pricing session, "would you have bought this?", a brutally honest scoreboard update.

**Ep 4 — "Flip It Till It Sells"**
- Hook: *"This was $3 and filthy. Watch."*
- Arc: The transformation format — clean / repair / restyle two or three items. Satisfying, ASMR-friendly, very sliceable.
- Product touchpoint: photographing the finished item well; using Auto Tags to draft the listing.
- Slices: cleaning ASMR, before/after reveal, "the $3 to $40 flip," a quick "how I photograph for listings" tip.

**Ep 5 — "My First Yard Sale Run"**
- Hook: *"Six yard sales, one Saturday, $40 in my pocket. Come with me."*
- Arc: Yard-sale marathon, cash-only chaos, the thrill of cheap volume buys. Mid-season scoreboard check — is she on pace for $1,000?
- Product touchpoint: filtering sales by type (yard sales), building a Saturday route.
- Slices: "come thrifting with me" POV, the running tally, best $1 find, the route map.

**Ep 6 — "What Actually Sells"**
- Hook: *"After 6 weeks I finally know what sells. Here's the list."*
- Arc: A reflective, educational episode. Her data so far — categories that move, what sits. Strong Shorts/Search performer.
- Product touchpoint: exporting her inventory to see what sold vs. sat; reading her own numbers.
- Slices: "5 things that always sell," "5 things that never do," the data reveal, a myth-bust.

**Ep 7 — "The Auction Gamble"**
- Hook: *"I've never done this before and I might lose all of it."*
- Arc: She tries sourcing from / participating in an auction. High tension, possible big win or loss.
- Product touchpoint: bidding on auction items; getting an outbid notification.
- Slices: the bidding-war clip, "did I win?", the nail-biter cutdown, scoreboard swing.

**Ep 8 — "Restock Day"**
- Hook: *"My shelves are empty. Time to spend everything I've made."*
- Arc: Reinvesting profits into a bigger sourcing run now that she has a budget. Shows the flywheel.
- Product touchpoint: discovery at scale — following multiple organizers, push notifications for new sales.
- Slices: the big haul, "reinvesting my profit," notification-to-purchase speedrun, a packed-shelf reveal.

**Ep 9 — "Sold Out Weekend"**
- Hook: *"I listed 30 things Friday. By Sunday, this is what's left."*
- Arc: A high-volume listing weekend using Rapid Capture to photograph a big batch fast. The payoff: a strong sales weekend.
- Product touchpoint: Rapid Capture batch photographing, Auto Tags review, publishing many items quickly.
- Slices: the Rapid Capture time-lapse ("30 items in 20 minutes"), the sold-notification montage, a "how I list fast" tip.

**Ep 10 — "Holds, Lowballers & Weird DMs"**
- Hook: *"Someone offered me $2 for a $60 lamp. Here's how it went."*
- Arc: The people side — managing holds, messages, lowball offers, funny buyer interactions. Light, relatable.
- Product touchpoint: managing holds/reservations and messaging buyers.
- Slices: "lowball offers I get," the funniest DM, "how holds work," a satisfied-buyer moment.

**Ep 11 — "Running My Own Sale"**
- Hook: *"I've got too much inventory. So I'm running my own sale."*
- Arc: The crossover — she flips from shopper to organizer and runs her own multi-item sell-off. Big production moment of the season.
- Product touchpoint: organizer side — creating a sale, Print Kit labels with QR codes, POS for in-person checkout, setting it live.
- Slices: "setting up my own sale," the Print Kit label run, POS checkout in action, the sale-day vlog.

**Ep 12 — "Did I Make $1,000?"**
- Hook: *"Twelve weeks ago I had $100. Here's the final number."*
- Arc: The finale. Final scoreboard reveal, what she learned, what's next. Sets up Season 2 / community Q&A.
- Product touchpoint: payout/earnings recap on the dashboard.
- Slices: the final-number reveal (the season's biggest hook), "everything I learned," a highlight montage, "should I keep going?" community-vote clip.

**Season A short-form bank (mix in anytime):** guess-the-sale-price, ASMR cleaning, "find of the day," route reveals, scoreboard updates, lowball-offer storytimes, fast-listing time-lapses.

# SEASON B — "Sourced"

**Premise:** The hunt as the story. Calm, aesthetic, "come with me" energy. The business grows quietly in the background; the foreground is discovery, objects, and the people behind them. Brand-safe, evergreen, searchable.

**Host voice:** Warm, curious, a storyteller. Treats objects like they have histories worth telling.

**Season-long spine:** "A season of finds" — a slowly filling shelf/wall of her best discoveries, revisited in the finale. No money pressure on screen; the reward is the object and the story.

**Recurring on-screen elements:** "Find of the Day," a one-line object history card, location title cards (the place is a character), and a calm closing "where it went" note.

### The 12 episodes

**Ep 1 — "Why I Started Hunting"**
- Hook (0–3s): *"Everything in this room used to belong to someone else. That's the whole point."*
- Arc: Gentle origin story. What drew her to second-hand. First find of the season. Sets the tone.
- Product touchpoint: opening the app to see what sales are happening this weekend near her.
- Slices: the mission-statement clip, "my favorite thrifted object," a 30s "how I find sales," a cozy listing-desk B-roll.

**Ep 2 — "5 Sales, One Saturday"**
- Hook: *"Five sales, one Saturday, one tank of gas. Let's see what's out there."*
- Arc: A sourcing marathon across estate, yard, and flea — but framed as a day out, not a grind.
- Product touchpoint: filtering by sale type and building a route for the day.
- Slices: "come thrifting with me" POV, the haul, best find, the map/route reveal.

**Ep 3 — "The Thing Nobody Wanted"**
- Hook: *"Everyone walked past this. I think it's the best thing in the room."*
- Arc: Deep dive on one overlooked object — its maker, era, materials, why it matters. Educational + emotional.
- Product touchpoint: checking an item's condition rating / details; a Crowd Appraisal on its value.
- Slices: the object-history micro-story, "would you have grabbed this?", a maker's-mark close-up, the appraisal reveal.

**Ep 4 — "Dead Malls & Flea Markets"**
- Hook: *"This mall has been dying since 1998. The flea market inside is alive."*
- Arc: Destination episode. Location as character. Atmospheric B-roll, vendor conversations, finds.
- Product touchpoint: discovering the flea market event and its hours/details in-app.
- Slices: the location reveal, a vendor's story, the best stall, a moody establishing-shot clip.

**Ep 5 — "Estate Sale Etiquette"**
- Hook: *"There are unwritten rules at estate sales. I learned them the hard way."*
- Arc: Soft-educational. How estate sales actually work, how to be respectful, how to find the good ones. High search value.
- Product touchpoint: finding well-run sales, following organizers she trusts.
- Slices: "estate sale dos and don'ts," "how to find the good ones," a first-timer tip, a haul.

**Ep 6 — "The Restoration"**
- Hook: *"This took me three days. Worth every minute."*
- Arc: A longer, satisfying restoration of one special piece. Process as meditation.
- Product touchpoint: photographing the finished piece; drafting the listing with Auto Tags.
- Slices: restoration ASMR, the before/after, a step-by-step tip, the finished-object beauty shot.

**Ep 7 — "Hunting With a Friend"**
- Hook: *"I brought someone who's never done this. Watch her catch the bug."*
- Arc: Bring a guest along (could be Patrick-adjacent or a friend). Social, fun, introduces the hobby to newcomers.
- Product touchpoint: sharing a sale link with a friend; both following it.
- Slices: "I took my friend thrifting," her first find, a reaction clip, the duo haul.

**Ep 8 — "What I Look For"**
- Hook: *"I can spot a good find in about two seconds. Here's how."*
- Arc: Her eye, taught. Materials, marks, quality tells. Strong evergreen/educational performer.
- Product touchpoint: searching for specific items/keywords across upcoming sales.
- Slices: "how to spot quality," "real vs fake," "always check the bottom," a quick search demo.

**Ep 9 — "A Trail of Treasures"**
- Hook: *"Three stops, one afternoon, all picked for me."*
- Arc: She follows a curated multi-stop route (estate sale → vintage shop → café). Experiential, charming.
- Product touchpoint: a Treasure Trail — checking in at each stop.
- Slices: "the perfect thrifting afternoon," each stop as a clip, the check-in moment, a cozy café closer.

**Ep 10 — "The Ones That Got Away"**
- Hook: *"I still think about the chair I didn't buy in March."*
- Arc: Reflective and funny — the regrets, the near-misses, the lessons. Very relatable to collectors.
- Product touchpoint: posting a Bounty for an item she's still hunting.
- Slices: "the one that got away," "post a bounty," a storytime, a community "what did you miss?" prompt.

**Ep 11 — "Setting Up My Own Sale"**
- Hook: *"My place is overflowing. Time to send these to new homes."*
- Arc: The crossover — she runs her own sale, framed as "rehoming" treasures rather than profit. The organizer tools shown gently.
- Product touchpoint: organizer side — creating the sale, Print Kit labels, sharing the QR code, going live.
- Slices: "setting up my first sale," the label/QR run, the share-card reveal, a sale-day vlog.

**Ep 12 — "A Season of Finds"**
- Hook: *"This shelf was empty in spring. Look at it now."*
- Arc: Finale montage. Revisit the season's best finds and where they went. Warm, full-circle, community Q&A.
- Product touchpoint: a recap of her Loot Log / collection.
- Slices: the shelf reveal, "my top finds of the season," a where-are-they-now clip, "what should I hunt next?" prompt.

**Season B short-form bank:** object-history micro-stories, ASMR cleaning/restoration, "find of the day," location reveals, "real vs fake" tells, calm listing-desk B-roll, café/road-trip texture clips.

# PATRICK'S TRACK — "FindA.Sale, Actually Used"

This is the **official-socials** track — the one Patrick shoots himself. Unlike the host seasons, here the product *is* the subject, but the style stays "shown in use, real workflow," not slideshow-ad. These map directly to the FAQ/help content already written and to the existing `claude_docs/strategy/guide-and-video-library-plan.md` (which already prioritizes the Rapid Capture, Review/Pricing, and Promotion clusters). Every video below answers a real FAQ, so the script is already 80% written — pull the answer text and demonstrate it on screen.

**Format:** screen-capture of the real product + a calm voiceover, OR a phone-over-the-shoulder shot of an actual sale being set up. 60–90s vertical for socials; stitch related ones into 5–8 min YouTube "how-to" explainers. No "AI" on screen — say **Auto Tags**, **Smart Pricing**, **Rapid Capture**.

**Two pillars, alternating weekly:** Organizer how-tos (the priority per the library plan) and Shopper how-tos.

### Organizer pillar (lead with these — they're the priority cluster)

1. **"Photograph 60 items in 20 minutes" (Rapid Capture)** — the flagship. Real sale, ⚡ Rapid Capture mode, batch shooting, then the Review queue. FAQ: *How does Rapid Capture work?* Hook: *"I photographed this entire room in under 20 minutes. Here's how."*
2. **"From photo to live listing" (Auto Tags + Review)** — one item from snapshot to published, showing how Auto Tags drafts the title/category/tags and how you edit them. FAQ: *How do Auto Tags work?*
3. **"How to price so it actually sells" (Smart Pricing vs. comparables vs. override)** — pricing workflow. Note: organizer's set price always wins over any suggestion. FAQ: *Tips for Success / pricing.*
4. **"Condition grades, with real examples"** — S–D grades shown against actual items. FAQ: *What is a Condition Rating?*
5. **"Print labels & QR stickers for your whole sale" (Print Kit)** — generate Avery labels + price sheet, print, apply. FAQ: *How do I use the Print Kit?*
6. **"Get paid: setting up payouts" (Stripe Connect)** — the Setup Payments flow, the $1 POS test, test cards. FAQ: *How do I get paid? / How do I test my payment setup?* Keep it calm and reassuring — money setup is where organizers get nervous.
7. **"Run in-person checkout from your phone" (POS)** — search inventory, add to cart, take card or cash, mark sold, real-time sync. FAQ: *How does Point of Sale work?*
8. **"Promote your sale everywhere" (Promote page + QR + share cards)** — the 8-platform Promote page, yard-sign QR, share-card themes. FAQ: *What is a QR code and how do I use it?*
9. **"Run an auction sale"** — enabling auctions, setting starting bids, how bidding closes. FAQ: *How do I run an auction sale?*
10. **"Going live: the pre-sale checklist"** — the Before-You-Go-Live walkthrough end to end. FAQ: *Pre-Sale Checklist.*

### Shopper pillar (alternate weeks)

11. **"Find sales near you in 30 seconds"** — map, search, filters, follow an organizer. FAQ: *How do I find sales near me?*
12. **"Set a hold before the sale opens"** — requesting and managing holds. FAQ: *What is a Hold?*
13. **"Scan to verify price & condition at the sale" (QR / Treasure Hunt scan)** — FAQ: *What is a Treasure Hunt scan?*
14. **"Never miss a find: follows + notifications"** — FAQ: *How do push notifications work?*
15. **"The Explorer's Guild, explained in 60 seconds"** — XP, the 5 ranks, what you unlock. FAQ: *What is the Explorer's Guild?* (Keep it light — this is the fun hook for shoppers.)
16. **"Post a bounty for the thing you can't find"** — FAQ: *What is a Bounty?*

**Production cadence for Patrick:** one organizer how-to + one shopper how-to per week, each cut to 60–90s vertical for all platforms. Every 4 weeks, stitch that month's organizer clips into one YouTube "Organizer Starter Guide" explainer and the shopper clips into a "Shopper Starter Guide." Because each maps to an existing FAQ answer, the voiceover script is a light edit of text that already exists.

**Why this track is the easy one:** no host to cast, no narrative arc to sustain, scripts already written in the FAQ, and it doubles as onboarding/help content you can link from inside the app and the help center.
