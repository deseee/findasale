## First-Comment Q&A (post as first comment ~30 min after launch to catch early traffic)

**Q: How is this different from just posting on Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist?**

> Those platforms are built for individual items. FindA.Sale is built for *events* — organizing hundreds of items at once, tagging them with QR codes, publishing a browsable catalog, and letting shoppers discover the sale before it opens. It's the difference between posting 200 individual listings and running a coordinated sale with a public-facing storefront.

**Q: Does this work for all sale types, or just estate sales?**

> Every type. Estate sales, yard sales, garage sales, auctions, flea markets, consignment — FindA.Sale was deliberately built for the full secondary sale market, not one niche. If you're organizing a multi-item sale of any kind, it works for you.

**Q: What does it cost?**

> Free tier is genuinely free — list a sale, tag items, and get discovered by shoppers at no cost. Simple is $9/month, Pro is $29/month, and Teams is $79/month. Paid tiers unlock higher item limits, bulk tools, analytics, and team features. No commission on sales.
