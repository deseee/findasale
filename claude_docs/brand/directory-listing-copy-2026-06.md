## Long description (paragraph)

FindA.Sale is the all-in-one platform for organizers who run secondary sales — estate sales, yard sales, garage sales, auctions, flea markets, and consignment shops. Snap a photo and the app auto-fills the title, description, tags, and an estimated value; print QR code labels for every item; and publish a browsable storefront so shoppers can find, favorite, and reserve items before the doors open. Organizers manage listings, payments, and paperwork in one place, with a built-in point-of-sale, one-click exports to eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, and EstateSales.net, and optional syndication of inventory to Google's free product listings. It's free to start — pay only when items sell — with paid plans for organizers running multiple or higher-volume sales.

## Pricing (VERIFIED LIVE 2026-06-12 — use these exact figures)

- **SIMPLE — Free.** No monthly cost; 10% platform fee when items sell (~13.2% total incl. ~3.2% card processing). 200 items/sale, 5 photos each, 100 auto-tags/mo, 1 active sale.
- **PRO — $29/mo.** 8% platform fee (~11.2% total). 3 concurrent sales, 500 items each, 10 photos/item, 2,000 auto-tags/mo, Smart Pricing, Virtual Queue, Flip Report, analytics, Brand Kit.
- **TEAMS — $79/mo.** 8% platform fee. Unlimited sales/items/photos/tags*, Command Center, Retail Mode, 5 team members (extra $20/mo each), webhooks, Market Hubs.
- **Single-sale upgrade — $9.99 one-time.** PRO-level capacity (500 items, 500 auto-tags, Flip Report, Virtual Queue) for one sale, no subscription. Same 10% fee as Simple.
- **Enterprise — from $500/mo** (annual): API access, white-label, custom integrations.

One-liner for listings: *"Free to start — just 10% when items sell. Paid plans $29 (Pro) and $79 (Teams)/mo lower fees to 8% and add bulk tools, analytics, and team features."*

## eBay / Google notes (why they belong in the copy)

- **eBay** is a first-class, front-of-pricing-page feature: one-click eBay CSV export on every tier, plus direct eBay Inventory API push on PRO. Mention eBay by name — it's a real differentiator and a search term organizers use.
- **Google product listings** = the shipped Google Merchant Center free-listings feed (#463, live). Organizer inventory that has shipping configured can appear in Google's free Shopping results. Phrase as "can syndicate" / "eligible inventory" — it's opt-in via the organizer's shipping setup, not automatic for every item.
